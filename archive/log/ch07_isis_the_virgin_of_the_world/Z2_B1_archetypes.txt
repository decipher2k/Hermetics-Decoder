Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Guarded Meaning
Nature is saturated with significance, but discloses itself by degrees.

2. Active Receptivity
The maternal principle transforms by receiving, carrying, and redistributing life.

3. Restorative Fidelity
Wisdom does not abandon fragments; it seeks, gathers, and restores them.

4. Alchemical Nature
Generation and healing proceed through corruption, motion, and purification.

5. Philosophical Unveiling
The veil is lifted by disciplined interpretation, not by external devotion alone.

6. Functional Multiplicity
Many divine names can designate one recurrent field of operation.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle reinforces one of the book's major interpretive laws: symbols become clearer when read as process and function rather than as isolated mythic decorations. Isis is one of the master keys to that law.
