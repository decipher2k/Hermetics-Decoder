Extraction

Central statements
- The Osiris-Typhon-Isis pattern clarifies fragmentation and regathering.
- Wisdom is shown as patient recollection under conditions of loss.

Operative qualities
Fragmentation, recollection, mourning, patience, and restorative fidelity.

Reformulation

The Osiris cycle sharpens the chapter by showing wisdom under conditions of loss. The active principle is enclosed, scattered, mutilated, or hidden; Isis responds not with abstraction but with patient search, mourning, regathering, and restoration. Receptive wisdom therefore includes fidelity to fragments.
