Extraction

Central statements
- Unveiling now appears as initiation into process rather than access to an object.
- The true servant of Isis is philosophical and disciplined.

Operative qualities
Initiatory unveiling, process-awareness, philosophical devotion, and earned access.

Reformulation

Unveiling in this chapter is not exposure of a hidden object. It is initiation into process. To know Isis is to understand the hidden operations of life, death, fertility, purification, and renewal. For this reason the chapter insists that her true follower must reason, inquire, and interpret rather than remain at the level of external worship.
