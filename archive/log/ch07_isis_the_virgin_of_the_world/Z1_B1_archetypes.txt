Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Veiled Totality
Reality is present in nature, but not immediately transparent to ordinary sight.

2. Receptive Potency
The maternal principle receives higher force and makes it generative.

3. Regathering Wisdom
What has been dispersed or slain can be sought out, recollected, and restored.

4. Process-Symbolism
Sacred emblems encode living operations of balance, corruption, nourishment, and renewal.

5. Medicinal Corruption
Decay can be the necessary threshold of regeneration.

6. Qualified Unveiling
Truth yields itself in proportion to readiness, discipline, and interpretive maturity.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle strengthens the book's overall logic of symbolic literacy by showing that natural imagery, ritual objects, and divine figures are not ornamental myth, but compressed process-descriptions of how life, wisdom, and transformation operate.
