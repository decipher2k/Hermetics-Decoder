Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter's rich emblematic detail encodes processes rather than static decorations.
- Robes, colors, instruments, animals, and vessels all describe transformation.

Operative qualities
Process-symbolism, transformation, elemental balance, purification, and circulation.

Reformulation

The chapter's emblems are process-signs. Her robes, colors, stars, grain, grapes, serpent, sistrum, scales, vessel, sacred fire, and attendant animals all encode cycles of growth, corruption, purification, balance, and renewal.

The symbols are therefore dynamic. Blackness indicates putrefaction and concealed generation. Gold indicates solar potency. Moisture receives and carries life. Rhythm and shaking restore movement. Scales indicate exact balance. Each emblem describes how life is sustained, corrected, and renewed.
