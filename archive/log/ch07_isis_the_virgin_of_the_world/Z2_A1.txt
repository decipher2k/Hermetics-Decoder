Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter is best read as a philosophy of veiled intelligibility.
- Isis names nature as inexhaustibly meaningful, but not transparently given.

Operative qualities
Veiled intelligibility, inexhaustibility, philosophical nature, and guarded meaning.

Reformulation

The chapter increasingly reads as a philosophy of veiled intelligibility. Isis represents a world that is saturated with meaning yet not immediately transparent. Nature discloses itself through symbol, fertility, rhythm, balance, and cyclic process, but it resists superficial possession.
