Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Veiled Intelligence
Nature is inwardly wise and cannot be exhausted by surface perception.

2. Receptive Agency
The maternal principle acts by receiving, carrying, balancing, and renewing.

3. Fragment-Gathering Fidelity
Restoration proceeds through patient recovery of what has been dispersed.

4. Regenerative Grammar
Elemental processes cooperate to heal, nourish, and transform.

5. Philosophical Unveiling
Truth is disclosed through disciplined interpretation and earned access.

6. Principial Recurrence
Different divine names may preserve one recurrent structure of operation.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle fixes Isis as one of the book's strongest keys to symbolic reading: divine forms must be read as active process-descriptions of nature, wisdom, and transformation rather than as static anthropomorphic personalities alone.
