Extraction

Central statements
- Receptivity is revealed as a mode of power.
- Nature mediates celestial force into embodied continuity.

Operative qualities
Mediating power, embodied continuity, receptive agency, and life transmission.

Reformulation

The chapter ultimately reveals receptivity as a mode of power. Isis mediates higher force into embodied continuity. She is the field through which life can be carried, distributed, and renewed without ceasing to depend on a source beyond itself.
