Extraction

Central statements
- The veiled figure makes concealment central to wisdom.
- Nature is intelligible, but only to the prepared interpreter.

Operative qualities
Concealed intelligibility, disciplined unveiling, reserve, and initiatory approach.

Reformulation

The veil of Isis is one of the chapter's decisive symbols. Nature is not opaque because it is meaningless; it is veiled because its meanings are layered and guarded. Unveiling is therefore neither crude exposure nor domination, but qualified interpretation.

The chapter's concern with initiates, secrecy, and the right to approach her sacred presence makes clear that wisdom is disclosed in proportion to readiness.
