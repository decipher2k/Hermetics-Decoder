Z2_A3

Extraction

Central statements
- Receptivity is active mediation, not inertia.
- The maternal principle generates by receiving and transforming higher influence.

Operative qualities
Active receptivity, mediation, transformation, and life-bearing moisture.

Reformulation

The chapter makes receptivity active. Isis does not merely wait upon external power. She receives, distributes, gestates, nourishes, stabilizes, and renews. Moisture, moon, field, robe, and maternal image all indicate a power of transformation that depends on reception without collapsing into passivity.
