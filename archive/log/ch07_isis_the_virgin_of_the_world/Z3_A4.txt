Central statements
- The Osiris cycle finalizes the theme of restorative search.
- Wisdom is faithful under fragmentation.

Operative qualities
Restorative search, fidelity, mourning, and continuity through recollection.

Reformulation

The Osiris cycle finalizes the chapter's theme of restorative search. Wisdom does not possess wholeness cheaply. It seeks what has been scattered, grieves what has been lost, and rebuilds continuity from fragments. This gives Isis a striking active fidelity within her receptive character.
