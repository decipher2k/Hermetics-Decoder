Refined archetypes after the second cycle:

1. Pattern-bearing sanctuary: a visible form that preserves invisible law.
2. Threshold intelligence: veils and grades as protections of reality.
3. Priestly embodiment: the mediator as a living synthesis of correspondences.
4. Portable holiness: sacred order carried through history and inward life.
5. Concentric simplification: the nearer the center, the fewer the forms.
6. Interpretive key: visible ritual requiring unwritten interior doctrine.
7. Uncoercible presence: divinity approached by worthiness, never compelled.
