Core archetypes emerging in the first cycle:

1. The portable cosmos: sacred order made transportable through measured form.
2. The sanctified body: the temple as an image of the human constitution.
3. Graded approach: sacrifice, purification, illumination, offering, silence.
4. The veiled center: highest presence concealed behind lawful thresholds.
5. Consecrated matter: substances differentiated by capacity to bear spirit.
6. Invested office: garments and gems as powers, not ornaments.
7. Dangerous holiness: access conditioned by moral adequacy.
