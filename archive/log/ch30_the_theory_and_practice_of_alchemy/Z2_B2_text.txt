This chapter develops alchemy into a comprehensive initiatory science. Hall links its origins to ancient revelation, its language to Qabbalistic and Hermetic symbolism, and its procedures to universal laws of sequence and correspondence. The process stages of the Great Work name thresholds through which matter, mind, and soul must pass. The Emerald Tablet gives the governing axiom that superior and inferior agree. The Rosicrucian allegory of the invisible mountain shows the same work in another register, where approach to the treasure requires danger, purification, courage, and guidance. The chapter insists that formulas are hidden not from caprice but because the operator himself must become adequate to the power he seeks.
