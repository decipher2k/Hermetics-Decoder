This chapter presents alchemy as a universal science of growth, regeneration, and intensified Nature. Hall argues that the same law governs spiritual rebirth, intellectual illumination, and material transmutation: what exists in seed can be multiplied and perfected by right art. Chemistry is therefore only alchemy's exterior husk, because the Hermetic art includes the invisible principles behind matter. Mercury, sulphur, salt, and Azoth are treated as powers spanning God, man, elements, and chemicals. The Great Work depends on analogy, on the doctrine that superior and inferior agree, and on the operator's own inward transformation, without which the decisive ingredient is absent.
