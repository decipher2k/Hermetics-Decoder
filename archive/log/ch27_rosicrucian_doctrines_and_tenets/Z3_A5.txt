The chapter's treatment of medicine and alchemy reaches its final clarity here. Elixir, panacea, transmutation, and prolonged life all remain possible, but their highest meaning is regenerative. Hall repeatedly redirects the reader from fascination with effects to the state of being that alone makes such effects safe. The worst disease is ignorance; the worst corruption is power without understanding.

Thus the rosy cross becomes the formula of medicine itself. The lower and higher natures must be conjoined, temporal life redeemed by eternal principle, and the divided human state healed from within. Every lesser cure is judged by whether it serves that integral restoration.

This gives the chapter a strongly ethical therapeutics that matches its social and spiritual ambitions.
