The three mountains of Sophia, Qabbalah, and Magia now provide a succinct ladder of attainment. Philosophy studies the workings of nature and begins ascent beyond the senses. Qabbalah teaches the celestial language and communion with higher intelligences. Magia crowns the ascent in knowledge imparted directly by God. Hall includes this scheme because it organizes Rosicrucian growth as ordered elevation rather than sudden illumination.

This threefold path helps interpret many of the chapter's themes: nature as text, sacred language as mediation, and divine knowledge as consummation. It also reinforces the Rosicrucian refusal of premature power. One climbs.

The doctrine is therefore developmental at every level: historical, intellectual, moral, and spiritual.
