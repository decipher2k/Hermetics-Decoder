The universal medicine becomes more intelligible in this pass. Hall allows both literal and symbolic readings, but the symbolic one is decisive: ignorance is the deepest disease, and the most potent cure is the understanding that restores man to reality. Physical healing is real and valued, yet it is a branch of a larger therapeutics aimed at races, nations, and souls.

This makes Rosicrucian medicine inseparable from philosophy and ethics. A cure that leaves ignorance intact would be incomplete. The panacea must heal the disorder of the whole human condition.

The fraternity's free offer of treasure and refusal of money thus make sense. It claims to distribute not commodities but a principle of restoration.
