Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Sourceward Ascent
Philosophy is the repeated effort to move from manifested multiplicity toward its formative ground.

2. Branching Differentiation
Schools proliferate because each develops one real access point into reality beyond its proper scope.

3. Concealed Genealogy
Public intellectual history rests upon a deeper sacred inheritance that is only partially visible in explicit doctrine.

4. Symbolic Endurance
Meaning survives historical fracture by entering forms that can carry depth without exposing it completely.

5. Form as Archive
Images, rites, myths, measures, and monuments function as repositories of doctrine in compressed and durable form.

6. Initiation into Reading
The chapter changes the reader's posture: understanding requires symbolic reconstruction, not information alone.

Relation to the whole work
The final cycle establishes the Introduction as the methodological threshold of the entire book. It instructs the reader to interpret every later chapter as visible diversity rooted in concealed continuity and preserved through enduring symbolic forms.
