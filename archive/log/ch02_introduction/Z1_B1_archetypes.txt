Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Ordering Ascent
Philosophy is the movement that rises from the crowded surface of things toward what grants them rank, cause, and proportion.

2. Fragmented Branching
Schools multiply because each seizes a partial entrance into reality and develops it into a world.

3. Hidden Ancestry
The public history of philosophy rests on a largely concealed sacred inheritance older than the schools themselves.

4. Guarded Transmission
What is most potent is not broadcast without condition; it passes through preparation, secrecy, and graded exposure.

5. Symbolic Durability
Visible form becomes the archive of invisible law when direct exposition is impossible, unsafe, or too fragile.

6. Readerly Initiation
The chapter does not only inform the reader about philosophy; it initiates a different way of reading history, doctrine, and image.

Relation to the whole work
These qualities make the Introduction the book's interpretive key. It transforms the coming chapters from a catalogue of antiquarian material into a coherent recovery of concealed wisdom embodied in diverse traditions.
