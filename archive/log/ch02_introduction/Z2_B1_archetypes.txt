Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Reordering Consciousness
Philosophy is not only a body of doctrines but a process by which attention is redirected from surface toward depth.

2. Branching Partiality
Historical schools preserve genuine insight, but each tends to absolutize one entrance into reality.

3. Hidden Trunk
Behind the visible history of philosophies stands a more primary sacred matrix from which they drew nourishment.

4. Delayed Disclosure
Truth is sometimes preserved best when placed into forms that wait for the right reader.

5. Durable Memory-Form
Myth, monument, ritual, and emblem function as memory devices that survive the collapse of explicit teaching.

6. Readerly Re-entry
The chapter positions the reader not as spectator of history but as one invited to re-enter depth through symbolic interpretation.

Relation to the whole work
This second cycle clarifies the Introduction as the book's gateway into method. It teaches that the rest of the text must be read as an archive of delayed disclosure rather than as disconnected antiquarian information.
