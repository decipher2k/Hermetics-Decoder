Extraction

Central statements
- CHiram is repeatedly universalized beyond the Biblical figure.
- He becomes cosmic martyr, archetypal man, and universal agent.
- The legend absorbs many prior traditions into one operative figure.

Operative qualities
Archetypal universality, synthesis, transferable identity, and cosmic personation.

Reformulation

The chapter continually expands CHiram beyond biography. He is aligned with Osiris, Hermes, Christ, Bacon, de Molay, solar power, alchemical fire, and the spirit in man. Each identification is less a historical claim than an effort to reveal the same operative pattern under many names.

This gives the legend enormous elasticity. CHiram becomes the archetypal builder-spirit, the principle of truth and beauty at work in the world and repeatedly struck down by lower forces.

The result is not confusion but concentration. Multiple traditions converge because the chapter reads them as local expressions of one regenerative drama.

The Hiramic figure thus functions as a universalized human and cosmic principle rather than a single person frozen in time.
