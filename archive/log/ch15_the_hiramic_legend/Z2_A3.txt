Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter binds solar, seasonal, and zodiacal motions to the Hiramic pattern.
- Cosmic rhythm and initiatory myth mirror one another.
- The legend is calendrical as well as moral and spiritual.

Operative qualities
Cosmic recurrence, seasonal death-and-return, stellar mirroring, and cyclical regeneration.

Reformulation

Another strong line of convergence appears in the astronomical reading of the legend. CHiram is the sun passing through assault, burial, stillness, and rising. The murderers become zodiacal or seasonal agencies; the raising becomes vernal recovery.

This does not displace the moral or initiatory meanings. It enlarges them. The same regenerative law is read in the heavens, in ritual, and in the soul.

The chapter therefore aligns human transformation with cosmic recurrence. Initiation is not an arbitrary invention but a participation in a larger rhythm of concealment and return.

The Hiramic pattern becomes universal because the cosmos itself repeats it.
