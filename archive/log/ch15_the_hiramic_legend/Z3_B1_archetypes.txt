Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Buried Builder-Ideal
Form and history conceal the creative principle that made them possible.

2. Salvific Beauty
Harmonious form educates and restores the soul toward higher order.

3. Lost Operative Relation
The true word is the living coordination by which higher and lower work together.

4. Analogical Recurrence
One regenerative pattern reappears across myths, stars, bodies, rituals, and institutions.

5. Unfinished Commonwealth
Civilization remains incomplete wherever truth and beauty lie unraised.

6. Redemptive Persistence
Though repeatedly struck down, the builder-spirit remains destined for recovery.

Relation to the whole work
This final cycle makes the Hiramic legend one of the book's most comprehensive interpretive templates, uniting dying-god myth, sacred architecture, initiatory anthropology, symbolic politics, and the eventual resurrection of hidden truth.
