Central statements
- The chapter's final horizon is an educative and symbolic commonwealth.
- The raising of CHiram culminates in universal instruction and rightly ordered society.
- Symbolism is treated as the supreme science of liberation.

Operative qualities
Illumined commonwealth, educative restoration, universal symbolism, and ordered freedom.

Reformulation

The closing vision of the chapter is remarkably expansive. The raising of CHiram culminates in a future civilization governed by illumination, proportion, and free access to the sciences of life. The temple becomes the university; the craft becomes universal education.

This makes symbolism the highest pedagogy. To learn that every visible thing is the tomb of an eternal principle is to recover the lost word in a new form.

The chapter therefore ends not in nostalgia but in program. Regeneration must become educational, social, and planetary.

CHiram is fully raised only when truth again becomes the master of work in human affairs.
