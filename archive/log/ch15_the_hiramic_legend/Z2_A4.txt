Operative qualities
Transmuting fire, universal agent, inner ascent, and alchemical construction.

Reformulation

The alchemical and physiological passages give the chapter another layer of depth. CHiram becomes the universal agent, the fiery airy water, the spirit fire that must be raised through inner degrees. Temple-building is thus recast as the preparation of the soul for transmutation.

This does not abandon the architectural symbolism. It radicalizes it. The true temple is built within, and its raising depends upon the disciplined ascent of a hidden force through ordered centers.

The lost word here becomes operative power: the right relation between force, center, and awakened intelligence.

The chapter therefore integrates Masonry with alchemy and inner regeneration under one constructive logic.
