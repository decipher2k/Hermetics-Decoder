Extraction

Central statements
- Beauty in the chapter is not decorative but salvific.
- Harmonious form reforms the soul and its environment.
- The beautifier and the redeemer are joined.

Operative qualities
Salvific beauty, proportioning influence, environmental formation, and redemptive form.

Reformulation

The chapter's long meditation on beauty becomes especially important here. CHiram is not only a murdered master; he is the beautifier of the eternal house. Beauty is treated as a power that educates, harmonizes, and redeems.

This means aesthetic order has initiatory force. Noble forms call forth nobility; asymmetry breeds inward disorder. Architecture, music, and art become active collaborators in regeneration.

The chapter therefore refuses any split between truth and beauty. The builder restores by beautifying, and beauty restores by making higher proportion lovable.

CHiram's labor is redemptive because it is formative.
