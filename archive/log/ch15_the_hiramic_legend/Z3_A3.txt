Extraction

Central statements
- The lost word is increasingly read as operative relation rather than secret syllable.
- Its concealment marks the interruption of right connection.
- Recovery means restored coordination of higher and lower orders.

Operative qualities
Lost relation, operative speech, restored coordination, and living key.

Reformulation

The lost word now reads less like a hidden password and more like a lost relation. It is the right coordination of spirit, form, labor, and consciousness by which the temple can truly be built and inhabited.

Its concealment with CHiram means that the lower world has lost the key to ordered cooperation with the higher. Ritual and initiation attempt to recover this key not as information alone but as living alignment.

The chapter thus turns speech into operation. The true word is effective because it restores relation.

The legend becomes the story of how that operative link is broken and renewed.
