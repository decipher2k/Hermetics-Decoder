Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter teaches that the true temple is unfinished wherever truth is unraised.
- Civilization itself is a worksite of initiation.
- Public order depends on recovered inner order.

Operative qualities
Unfinished temple, civilizational initiation, public consequence, and collective alignment.

Reformulation

The temple becomes most powerful in the chapter when read as civilization in process. Wherever truth is buried, beauty suppressed, and the builder-spirit obstructed, the house remains unfinished no matter how grand its outer form.

This extends initiation into public life. A people, institution, or age must raise its own hidden CHiram if it is to become proportionate and free.

The chapter therefore rejects private enlightenment severed from collective consequence. Inner order must eventually take public form.

Civilization itself becomes a worksite of regeneration.
