Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. The Builder-Spirit
Higher formative intelligence labors to erect enduring vessels of truth and order.

2. Silent Construction
Sacred form arises through proportionate preparation rather than contentious force.

3. Universal Martyrdom
One regenerative pattern appears under many names across traditions and histories.

4. Threshold Assault
Lower powers attack the gates through which higher reality seeks embodiment.

5. Hidden Survival
Living force persists through burial, sign, and awaiting retrieval.

6. Beautifying Restoration
The raising of truth renews art, education, community, and inner life together.

Relation to the whole work
This first cycle makes the Hiramic legend one of the book's most concentrated regeneration myths, uniting temple symbolism, dying-god patterns, and the recovery of hidden wisdom in both soul and civilization.
