Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Wisdom in Rupture
Higher truth persists through burial, ritual, and form even when its bearer is struck down.

2. Inward Temple
Outer legend mirrors a psychospiritual drama within the human constitution.

3. Cosmic Repetition
Seasonal and stellar motions replay the same law of death and return.

4. Alchemical Builder
Constructive labor becomes inner transmutation through awakened force.

5. Persecuted Principle
State, church, and crowd repeatedly suppress the powers that would illumine them.

6. Symbolic Commonwealth
The full raising of truth culminates in universal education and rightly ordered society.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle makes the Hiramic legend a major synthetic chapter, gathering earlier themes of dying gods, sacred architecture, hidden knowledge, cosmic recurrence, and regeneration into a single transferable initiatory pattern.
