Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Embodied Initiation
Sacred environments can enact transformation rather than merely describe it.

2. Operative Geometry
Number and proportion guide consciousness because they participate in intelligible order.

3. Guarded Readiness
Thresholds protect stages of becoming by restricting premature access.

4. Living Apex
The transformed human being is the capstone and completion of the temple.

5. Death-Proof Knowledge
Second death yields certainty of higher life through direct passage.

6. Enduring Compression
When rites disappear, architecture can still retain a compressed witness to their science.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle fixes the Pyramid as one of the strongest demonstrations in the book that hidden wisdom may be preserved in exact form: not only in texts and symbols, but in spaces designed to shape consciousness, test readiness, and effect rebirth.
