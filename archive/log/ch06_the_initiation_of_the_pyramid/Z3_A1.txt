Central statements

Operative qualities

Reformulation

The chapter's final center is sacred geometry as initiatory method. The Great Pyramid does not simply symbolize transformation from a distance. It embodies transformation in space, proportion, and sequence so that the candidate can undergo rather than merely contemplate the doctrine.
