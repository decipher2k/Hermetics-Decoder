Extraction

Central statements
- The capstone image turns the initiate into the completion of the temple.
- Human perfection is architectural and focal.

Operative qualities
Completion, focalization, inner shaping, and living apex.

Reformulation

One of the chapter's most powerful symbolic developments is the capstone. The temple is not truly complete apart from the transformed initiate. As rough stone becomes trued and fitted, so the human being is shaped by the Mysteries into the living apex through which higher power can descend into the structure below.

This makes perfection dynamic. The temple teaches completion by making the initiate itself part of the architecture.
