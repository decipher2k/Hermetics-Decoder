Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter opposes antiquarian and funerary explanations in order to restore sacred function.
- Surface history obscures inner purpose.

Operative qualities
Restored function, polemical clearing, hidden purpose, and shell versus meaning.

Reformulation

The chapter repeatedly clears away explanations that reduce the Pyramid to exterior use. Tomb theory, dynastic attribution, casual historicism, biblical speculation, and merely technical fascination all appear as partial or misleading approaches.

This negative work matters because it establishes one of the chapter's strongest tensions: shell versus meaning. The visible structure survives, but its living use has largely vanished from public memory. The world sees a stone shell and mistakes it for a dead object. The chapter tries to restore the hidden function that once animated it.
