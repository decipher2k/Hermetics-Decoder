Z2_A2

Extraction

Central statements
- Geometry is treated as revelation, not abstraction alone.
- Orientation, number, and proportion are initiatory intelligences.

Operative qualities
Geometric revelation, orientation, proportional intelligence, and symbolic exactitude.

Reformulation

The chapter gives geometry revelatory force. Orientation to the cardinal directions, numerical relations, chamber placements, and proportional harmonies are not decorative refinements. They express intelligible order in a way the initiate can inhabit.

The Pyramid teaches through exactitude.
