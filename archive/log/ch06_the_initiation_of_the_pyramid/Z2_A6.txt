Extraction

Central statements
- The King's Chamber is the enacted center of ontological transition.
- Second death is experiential proof rather than speculative doctrine.

Operative qualities
Ontological transition, second death, proof through passage, and certainty.

Reformulation

The King's Chamber increasingly appears as the enacted center of ontological transition. Here the initiate does not reason abstractly about immortality; he passes through an ordeal meant to render the distinction between transient body and enduring life experientially certain.

The chapter's spiritual science depends on this passage from belief to proof.
