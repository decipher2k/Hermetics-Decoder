Extraction

Central statements
- Hidden entrances and lost routes emphasize guarded access.
- The Pyramid is a school of threshold discipline.

Operative qualities
Guarded access, threshold discipline, secrecy, and prepared approach.

Reformulation

The chapter's fascination with blocked passages, unknown entrances, sealed ways, and subterranean access is more than archaeological. It supports the claim that the Pyramid was a school of threshold discipline. One did not wander casually into its highest uses. Access was guarded because transformation required preparation.
