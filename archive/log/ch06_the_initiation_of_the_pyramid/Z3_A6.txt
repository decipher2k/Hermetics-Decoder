Reformulation

The second death remains the chapter's decisive experiential pivot. The King's Chamber is where metaphysical instruction becomes irreversible knowledge through stillness, cold, enclosure, suspension, and separation from ordinary identity.

The reborn one emerges with certainty rather than opinion.
