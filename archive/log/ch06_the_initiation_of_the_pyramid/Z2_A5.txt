Central statements
- The capstone doctrine now clarifies the initiate's role as completed form.
- Human formation is the interior counterpart of architectural finishing.

Operative qualities
Completed form, inner finishing, apex-consciousness, and focused descent.

Reformulation

The capstone symbolism sharpens in this cycle. The initiate is not merely a visitor to the temple, but its intended interior counterpart. As the unfinished stone is trued, so the human being is inwardly finished into a form capable of receiving and focusing descending wisdom.

Completion is therefore participatory.
