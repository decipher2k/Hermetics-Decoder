Extraction

Central statements
- Passage through the Pyramid enacts rebirth.
- Initiation is architectural movement through concealed order.

Operative qualities
Ritual passage, rebirth, concealed order, and spatial transformation.

Reformulation

The chapter's initiatory doctrine is spatial. One does not merely hear truth in the Pyramid; one moves through it. Hidden corridors, sealed thresholds, chambers, descents, ascents, and crossings all become stages in a controlled transformation.

Architecture is therefore ritualized. The candidate's body travels through a designed sequence while the soul is prepared for detachment, deathlike ordeal, celestial knowledge, and second birth. Space itself teaches.
