Reformulation

The chapter increasingly reads as a defense of sacred function. Its repeated arguments against tomb theory, casual history, and merely technical explanation are meant to restore a truth that the structure itself still bears, even if the ritual culture around it has vanished.

The Pyramid survives because its meaning was built into form.
