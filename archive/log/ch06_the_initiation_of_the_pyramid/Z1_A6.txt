Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter places deathlike experience at the center of illumination.
- The King's Chamber is threshold, not burial room.

Operative qualities
Threshold death, suspension, transcendence, and experiential certainty.

Reformulation

The King's Chamber is interpreted not as a place of interment but as a threshold of experiential certainty. The candidate undergoes a second death, lies in the coffer, and through separation from bodily condition learns directly the difference between mortality and life.

Deathlike cold, unusual sound, enclosed stillness, and cosmic orientation all contribute to this chamber as a crossing point between the sensible world and transcendental realization.
