Z1_A3

Extraction

Central statements
- The Pyramid is a cosmogram and anthropogram simultaneously.
- Macrocosm and microcosm are built into its geometry.

Operative qualities
Correspondence, cosmic order, embodied analogy, and geometric revelation.

Reformulation

The chapter treats the Pyramid as both cosmogram and anthropogram. Its lines, chambers, orientations, and proportions represent the order of the world and the structure of the human constitution together.

The base signifies material nature, elemental embodiment, and lawful foundation. The rising faces signify the upward powers of spiritual life. The chambers correspond to central organs and inner centers. The capstone signifies concentrated completion. In this way the building becomes the human being transposed into architecture and the universe condensed into habitable symbolic form.
