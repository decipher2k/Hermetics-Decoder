Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Formal Witness
Sacred function can survive historical loss when embedded in durable design.

2. Geometric Intelligence
Number, orientation, and proportion disclose order experientially.

3. Threshold Discipline
Access to transformation is structured, guarded, and sequential.

4. Interior Completion
The initiate becomes the finished form toward which the temple points.

5. Experiential Certainty
Second death converts metaphysical claim into direct knowledge.

6. Compressed Continuity
An emptied structure can still carry a living remainder of the rites it once housed.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle clarifies one of the book's largest strategies: hidden law survives not only in texts and myths, but in built environments whose form can outlast the full disappearance of their original ceremonial life.
