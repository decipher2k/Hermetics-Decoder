Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Doctrinal Architecture
Truth can be built into stone, proportion, and passage.

2. Hidden Function
The visible shell conceals a deeper initiatory use.

3. Spatial Transformation
Movement through designed space becomes a mode of inner change.

4. Living Completion
The initiate is shaped into the apex that completes the structure.

5. Threshold Death
Illumination requires passage through a deathlike suspension.

6. Enduring Covenant
Even emptied of active rites, sacred form can continue bearing witness to eternal wisdom.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle clarifies how the book's general claims about symbolism, initiation, and preserved hidden law can take full architectural embodiment. The Pyramid is one of the work's clearest demonstrations that form itself may carry transformation.
