Extraction

Central statements
- The structure mirrors both cosmos and person.
- Architecture becomes an externalized anatomy of transformation.

Operative qualities
Mirroring, externalized anatomy, cosmic-human correspondence, and formative structure.

Reformulation

The Pyramid mirrors both cosmos and person. It is an externalized anatomy of transformation. By entering it, the initiate enters a structured likeness of the world and of the soul. This is why the chapter insists so strongly on its symbolic chambers, passages, and levels.

The building teaches because it is a body of correspondences.
