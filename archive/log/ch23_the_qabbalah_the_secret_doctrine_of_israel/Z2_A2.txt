The dominant law in pass two is reflection with diminishment.

- Atziluth reflects into Briah.
- Briah reflects into Yetzirah.
- Yetzirah reflects into Assiah.
- Divine names become archangels, then hierarchies, then planetary and elemental orders.
- Pure powers appear below as weaker, more bounded, and more material expressions.

The chapter thus provides one of the book's clearest accounts of how transcendence can remain present through mediation without collapsing into identity with its lowest forms.
