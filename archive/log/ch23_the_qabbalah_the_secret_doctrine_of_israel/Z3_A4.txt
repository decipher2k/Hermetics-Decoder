Final transmutation:

Hall's Qabbalah is an architectonic metaphysics. It treats existence as a carefully ordered descent from the unconditioned into name, number, letter, world, hierarchy, body, and text, without ever severing the lower from the higher. Every level is a vessel, every vessel both contains and limits, and every limitation makes relation visible. The invisible is therefore not absent from form; it is stratified within it.

At the same time, the chapter turns hermeneutics into spiritual ascent. If sacred writings are built in the same way worlds are built, then interpretation becomes a reverse cosmogony: reading upward through letter, number, image, and myth toward the interior law from which they descend. The Qabbalist is thus not merely a commentator, but a climber of worlds.
