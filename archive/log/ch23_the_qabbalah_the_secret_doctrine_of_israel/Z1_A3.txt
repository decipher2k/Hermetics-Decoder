Primary symbolic clusters in pass one:

- Three degrees of law and hidden teaching.
- Sepher Yetzirah as a grammar of creation through numbers, letters, and sounds.
- AIN, AIN SOPH, and AIN SOPH AUR as progressively apprehensible modes of the unconditioned.
- Four worlds and forty spheres as descending differentiations of power.
- Sephirothic Tree, pillars, paths, and divine names.
- Adam Kadmon, microcosm, androgyne, fall, and re-ascent.
- Gematria, Notarikon, and Temurah as hermeneutic instruments.

Together these show a universe read as text and a text read as universe.
