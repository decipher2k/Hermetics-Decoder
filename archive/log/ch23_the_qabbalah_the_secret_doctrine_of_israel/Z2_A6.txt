Hall uses the Qabbalah to disclose a universe structured by inclusion, reflection, and graduated mediation. The unconditioned source establishes a first point, and from it descend worlds, powers, hierarchies, names, letters, and bodies in diminishing degrees of purity. The Sephirothic Tree condenses this entire process into one diagram of balanced relation, while Adam Kadmon and the microcosmic man reveal that the same order is present in human constitution.

The chapter's hermeneutic force is equally strong: sacred writings conceal this architecture under narrative, law, and anthropomorphic imagery. Qabbalah is therefore both cosmology and the science of reading hidden structure.
