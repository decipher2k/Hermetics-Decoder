Second pass clarifies that the chapter is organized by nestedness. Everything is inside something greater while still containing lesser forms within itself. The worlds are inside the primordial point; the Sephiroth interpenetrate; man stands within the cosmic egg while containing reflections of all levels; scripture contains unwritten doctrine; sacred names contain powers; letters contain worlds. The chapter's logic is therefore enclosure and inclusion rather than linear sequencing alone.

This nested structure explains why Hall repeatedly uses images of ring, tree, egg, body, veil, garment, and vessel.
