Second-pass clarification of special forms:

- AIN SOPH: unconditioned source beyond positive definition.
- Kether and first point: primal concentration without total separation from source.
- Four worlds: descending degrees of manifestation.
- Sephiroth: vessels or powers through which relation becomes structured.
- Adam Kadmon: universal man as living compendium of all levels.
- Gematria, Notarikon, Temurah: methods by which language reveals concealed architecture.
- Fall and divided sexuality: metaphysical disunion and the task of restored equilibrium.

These forms show the chapter as both map of being and method of return.
