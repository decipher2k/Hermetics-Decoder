First transmutation:

The chapter's central labor is to render hidden order diagrammatic. The Qabbalah makes thinkable how the indefinable can still generate structure without ceasing to transcend it. The unconditioned does not become finite by abandoning itself, but by establishing points, vessels, names, letters, and paths through which its power is reflected downward in diminishing brightness. Each level is real, each more conditioned than the last, and each still rooted in what surpasses it.

The chapter also insists that exegesis must mirror cosmogony. If reality descends by veils and gradations, truth must be recovered by reading through veils and gradations. Hence hidden doctrine is not an optional embellishment of scripture but the only way the text becomes proportionate to the world it claims to disclose.
