Final structural laws:

1. Truth is layered and concealed within outer forms.
2. Creation proceeds by graded emanation, reflection, and limitation.
3. Letters, numbers, and names are operative principles of form.
4. Balanced polarity requires a mediating center.
5. Man is the microcosmic recapitulation of the whole order.
6. Literal reading obscures what symbolic reading restores.
7. Ascent is possible because the source remains present through every level.

These laws make the chapter one of the book's decisive methodological pivots.
