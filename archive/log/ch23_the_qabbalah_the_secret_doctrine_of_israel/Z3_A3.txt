Final clarification of major symbols:

- Soul of the soul of the law: deepest interior of revelation.
- Sepher Yetzirah: formative grammar of creation.
- AIN SOPH and the first point: transcendence and initial self-limitation.
- Four worlds and forty spheres: nested descent of power.
- Tree of Sephiroth: equilibrium, hierarchy, path, and return.
- Gematria, Notarikon, Temurah: techniques of unveiling concealed order.
- Adam Kadmon and Adam/Eve: universal wholeness, divided embodiment, and recovery.

These symbols are strongest when read as interlocking structures rather than isolated doctrines.
