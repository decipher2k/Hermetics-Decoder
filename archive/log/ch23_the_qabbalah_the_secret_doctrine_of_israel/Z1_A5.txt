What must be avoided:

- Treating the chapter as only historical reportage on Jewish mysticism.
- Collapsing its highly structured metaphysics into vague monism.
- Missing that the chapter offers both a cosmology and a method of reading.
- Reducing the symbolic diagrams to decorative occultism.

What must be preserved:

- Layered revelation.
- Emanation through levels.
- Letters and numbers as formative powers.
- Man as microcosm and path of return.
- The Tree as relational architecture, not isolated symbolism.
