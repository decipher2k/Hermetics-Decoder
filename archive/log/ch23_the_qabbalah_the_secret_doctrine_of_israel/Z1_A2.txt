First reduction yields six governing operations.

1. Revelation is stratified and requires initiatory reading.
2. Creation unfolds by graded emanation from the unconditioned into increasingly bounded worlds.
3. Number, letter, sound, and name are structural powers, not arbitrary signs.
4. The Sephirothic Tree maps relations among divine powers, cosmic levels, and human constitution.
5. Scripture conceals metaphysical process beneath historical and anthropomorphic surfaces.
6. Man is a microcosmic recapitulation of the whole emanative order and can reascend through it.

The chapter is therefore both cosmology and method of decipherment.
