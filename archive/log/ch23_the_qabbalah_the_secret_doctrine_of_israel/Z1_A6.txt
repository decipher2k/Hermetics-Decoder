Recomposition sketch:

Hall presents the Qabbalah as the hidden doctrine of Israel and as one of the most comprehensive symbolic systems ever devised. It teaches that sacred law has outer, inner, and most interior meanings; that creation proceeds by graded emanation from the unconditioned into successively more bounded worlds; and that numbers, letters, sounds, and divine names are the operative grammar of this descent. The Sepher Yetzirah, the doctrine of AIN SOPH, the four worlds, the Sephirothic Tree, and the figure of Adam Kadmon all serve to map the relation between divine source, cosmic process, scripture, and man.

The chapter's deeper force lies in its union of metaphysics and interpretation: the same structure that produces worlds also conceals truth in texts and bodies. Qabbalah thus becomes a method for reading the universe and the self.
