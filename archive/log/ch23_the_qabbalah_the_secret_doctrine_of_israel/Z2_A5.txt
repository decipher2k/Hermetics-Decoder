The chapter also sharpens a major methodological principle of the entire project: literalism is a veil. Biblical figures, laws, objects, and narratives repeatedly signify powers, structures, and processes rather than merely historical events or personalities. Hall's treatment of Elohim, Adam, Eden, the cherubim, the ark, and Mosaic texts makes this explicit. The text becomes spiritually useful only when anthropomorphic surfaces are passed through.
