Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter treats disorder as mistuned relation rather than independent principle.
- Discord and deformity are the lowest degrees of harmony.
- Evil is reframed as diminished consonance.

Operative qualities
Mistuning, diminished consonance, corrective diagnosis, and graded disorder.

Reformulation

Another important clarification lies in the chapter's treatment of evil and deformity. Disorder is not granted independent status. It is understood as the least harmonious combination, the diminished or distorted relation of elements otherwise rooted in the Good.

This gives the chapter a distinct metaphysical optimism. Correction is possible because discord is not ultimate; it is mistuning.

The therapeutic emphasis of music and color depends on this assumption. What has fallen out of ratio can be restored.

The chapter therefore reframes spiritual and emotional disorder as a problem of relation, proportion, and tuning.
