Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter presents cosmos as scale, octave, and graduated field.
- Planets, elements, and notes are arranged through recurring ratios.
- Universal order is stepwise rather than chaotic.

Operative qualities
Graduation, octave-law, recurrent interval, and scalar cosmology.

Reformulation

The chapter's cosmic theory becomes more unified when read through the idea of scale. Universe, elements, planets, modes, and colors all unfold through graduated steps rather than undifferentiated spread.

The octave becomes especially important because it gives recurrence without repetition. Higher and lower correspond without collapsing into sameness.

This scalar logic explains why so many systems can be coordinated. The same law of interval structures multiple domains.

The chapter therefore presents the cosmos as a graduated ladder of resonant correspondences.
