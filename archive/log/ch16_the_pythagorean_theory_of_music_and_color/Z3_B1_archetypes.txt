Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Formative Harmony
Beauty is the Good becoming active through proportionate sound and light.

2. Initiated Perception
Hearing and sight must be refined to receive symbolic order consciously.

3. Recurring Proportion
One law repeats across tone, color, planet, element, and structure without collapse into identity.

4. Resonant Efficacy
Vibration acts therapeutically, ritually, and cosmologically.

5. Civic Aesthesis
Sensory form shapes character and therefore culture and polity.

6. Participatory Praise
Restored harmony culminates in conscious joining of the cosmic order.

Relation to the whole work
This final cycle makes the chapter a major methodological and practical hinge: it explains how proportion becomes sensuous, how symbolism can heal and educate, and how beauty links inner restoration with cosmic and civic order.
