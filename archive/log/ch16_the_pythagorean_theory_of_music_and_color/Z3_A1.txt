Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter resolves into harmony as a universal formative medium.
- Sound and light are not examples of order alone but vehicles by which order works.
- Aesthetics becomes ontology in motion.

Operative qualities
Formative medium, operative beauty, moving ontology, and sensuous intelligibility.

Reformulation

On the third pass, the chapter's deepest unity appears in its treatment of harmony as a formative medium. Sound and light do not merely display order. They are among the means by which order enters experience, shapes life, and restores proportion.

This makes aesthetics ontological. Beauty is no superficial addition to reality. It is reality's own goodness becoming effective in motion, tone, and radiance.

The chapter therefore elevates music and color from arts of pleasure to instruments of participation in the real.

Harmony becomes one of the living media of being.
