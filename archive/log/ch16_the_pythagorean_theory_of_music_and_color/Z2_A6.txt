Central statements

Operative qualities
Visible music, spectral pedagogy, symbolic radiance, and chromatic intelligence.

Reformulation

The treatment of color becomes richer when seen as visible music. The spectrum functions like a scale, with intervals, recurrences, and correspondences to planets, zodiacal divisions, and human principles.

This means light does more than illuminate external things. It instructs through its own differentiated order.

Color is therefore pedagogical. It teaches gradation, relation, and the deployment of force across visible manifestation.

The chapter turns chromatics into a school of symbolic intelligence.
