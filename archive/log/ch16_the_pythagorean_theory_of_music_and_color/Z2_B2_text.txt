The chapter can now be read as an account of harmony as attuning law. Music and color matter because they actively bring soul, body, and perception into right relation.

This law is both exact and lived. Pythagorean ratios give form to harmony, but harmony proves itself in its effects: healing, composure, moral formation, and perceptual refinement.

The chapter also extends harmony across media. Architecture becomes frozen chord, sound becomes temporal structure, and the cosmos itself appears as a graduated scale of resonant intervals.

Discord is therefore not an ultimate principle but mistuning, something susceptible to correction. Color deepens this teaching by making proportion visible.

The chapter's lasting claim is that education in harmony is education in reality itself.
