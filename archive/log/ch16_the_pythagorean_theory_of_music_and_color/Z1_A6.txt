Extraction

Central statements
- Color is presented as vibration, revelation, and symbolic language.
- Chromatic order parallels musical order and cosmological structure.
- Light unfolds as differentiated creative powers.

Operative qualities
Chromatic vibration, revelatory light, differentiated radiance, and visible harmony.

Reformulation

The philosophy of color extends the same principles into vision. Light is the primary physical manifestation of life, and color is light differentiated into ordered vibratory qualities. Like tones, colors form a lawful scale.

The chapter treats this scale as both scientific and symbolic. Cities, towers, fountains, zodiacal wheels, and esoteric anthropologies all employ color as a language of structured powers.

Color therefore is not decorative surface. It is visible harmony, a spectrum through which creative attributes become perceptible.

The chapter turns light into another mode of proportional revelation.
