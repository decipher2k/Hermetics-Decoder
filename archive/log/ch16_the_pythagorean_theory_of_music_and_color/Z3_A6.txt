Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter's final movement is toward praise and reintegration.
- When freed from grossness, the soul hears and joins the cosmic anthem.
- Harmony culminates in participation rather than observation alone.

Operative qualities
Participatory praise, reintegrated hearing, cosmic fellowship, and restored consonance.

Reformulation

At its highest point, the chapter becomes a doctrine of reintegration. The music of the spheres is not merely something to be theorized about. It is something the soul may once again hear and join when freed from the coarseness of lower limitation.

This gives harmony a liturgical culmination. To be restored is to participate in the universal chant rather than stand apart from it.

The chapter therefore moves from analysis to communion. Order is finally not only known but shared.

Harmony culminates in praise.
