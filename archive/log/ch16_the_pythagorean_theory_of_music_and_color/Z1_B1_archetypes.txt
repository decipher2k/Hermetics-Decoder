Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Manifest Harmony
Beauty appears wherever proportionate relation becomes sensibly present.

2. Resonant Body
The human constitution is an instrument whose order or disorder can sound forth.

3. Mathematical Consonance
Pythagorean ratio gives harmony formal and transferable exactness.

4. Therapeutic Modulation
Sound restores, redirects, or disturbs inner life according to mode and proportion.

5. Celestial Song
Cosmic motion participates in an ordered audible relation.

6. Chromatic Revelation
Light differentiates into visible powers that parallel tone and structure.

Relation to the whole work
This first cycle makes the chapter a major bridge between Pythagorean mathematics and later symbolic chapters by showing how ratio becomes audible, visible, curative, and cosmological at once.
