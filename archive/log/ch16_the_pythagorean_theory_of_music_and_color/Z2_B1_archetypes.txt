Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Attuning Law
Harmony actively brings parts into right relation rather than merely describing them.

2. Integrated Knowing
True proportion is both mathematically exact and experientially effective.

3. Frozen Chord
Architecture and form are transposed expressions of vibratory order.

4. Scalar Cosmos
Universe unfolds through repeated intervals and graduated octaves.

5. Correctable Discord
Disorder is mistuning and therefore susceptible to restoration.

6. Visible Music
Color educates through the same proportional logic as tone.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle makes the chapter one of the book's key methodological bridges by showing that harmony can be read, heard, seen, built, and therapeutically applied across nearly every symbolic domain Hall engages.
