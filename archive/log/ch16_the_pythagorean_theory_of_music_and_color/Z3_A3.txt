Central statements

Operative qualities
Recurring law, structural analogy, dynamic repetition, and proportional transference.

Reformulation

The many correspondences in the chapter are best read as recurrent law rather than simple identity. Red is not merely the same thing as a note or a planet; it occupies a structurally corresponding place within a proportional system.

This is an important methodological refinement. Analogy in the chapter is dynamic and positional. Domains mirror one another through recurrence of relation.

That is why octaves, spectra, planets, and elements can be brought into conversation without being collapsed into sameness.

The chapter therefore offers a disciplined model of correspondential thinking.
