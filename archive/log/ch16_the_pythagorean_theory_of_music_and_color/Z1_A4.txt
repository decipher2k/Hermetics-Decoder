Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter repeatedly links music to therapy and emotional regulation.
- Modes, rhythms, and tonalities are treated as remedies or provocations.
- Harmony acts directly upon soul and body.

Operative qualities
Therapeutic modulation, affective governance, restorative sound, and psychophysical tuning.

Reformulation

Another strong line in the chapter is its insistence that sound acts upon the living being. Music is medicine because modes, tempos, and tonal structures directly influence emotion, passion, memory, and bodily condition.

This makes harmony practical rather than speculative. One mode excites, another calms, another restores proportion to a disturbed interior state. The anecdotes about Pythagoras and his disciples illustrate this law through concrete intervention.

The chapter therefore treats music as a disciplined technology of soul-tuning.

Sound becomes one of the clearest means by which order may be restored where inner balance has been lost.
