Final structural laws:

1. Sacred generation must be read without vulgar reduction.
2. Growth is an initiatory sequence.
3. Beauty is reproductive law transfigured.
4. The tree is the archetype of one source expressing itself as ordered multiplicity.
5. Utility, medicine, and metaphysics reinforce each other in plant symbolism.
6. Immortality is repeatedly imaged through recurrence, evergreen continuity, and hidden seed.

These laws make the chapter one of the book's clearest studies in symbolic process.
