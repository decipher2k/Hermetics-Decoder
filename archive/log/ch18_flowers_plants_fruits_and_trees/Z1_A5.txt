What must be avoided:

- Treating the opening phallic discussion as sensational rather than methodological.
- Separating floral symbolism from the larger doctrine of spiritual unfoldment.
- Reading tree worship as primitive idolatry instead of analogical cosmology.
- Missing how medicine, magic, ritual, and theology are integrated in vegetal symbols.

What must be preserved:

- Generation dignified as sacred process.
- Growth as a model of initiation.
- The tree as a master diagram of cosmos and man.
- Plants as both useful and symbolic, practical and metaphysical.
