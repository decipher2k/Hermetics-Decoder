Recomposed second-pass statement:

Hall uses the vegetal world to restore sacred meaning to generation, growth, and recurrence. Flowers express beauty as disciplined fertility; the lotus and rose show how latent life opens in ordered degrees; trees become diagrams of cosmos, man, lineage, and balance; fruits and magical herbs reveal how nourishment, temptation, ecstasy, healing, and occult force all belong to the same living field. The chapter insists that growth itself is intelligible and therefore symbolic.

Plant life is sacred because it translates hidden law into visible sequence.
