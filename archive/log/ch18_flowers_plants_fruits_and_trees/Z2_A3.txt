The tree now stands out as the chapter's major synthetic figure. It combines cosmology, genealogy, anthropology, and doctrine of equilibrium.

As cosmos, it is the world-axis, the support of spheres, and the branching of effects from one hidden source. As man, it is the body growing from invisible root and expressing itself in differentiated faculties. As doctrine, it distinguishes balance from polarity through the paired trees of life and of knowledge. As transmission, it becomes the family tree, the order of sages, the staff, the pillar, and the wooden support of sacred building.

The tree is therefore not one symbol among others, but a structural model for how the whole symbolic universe is organized.
