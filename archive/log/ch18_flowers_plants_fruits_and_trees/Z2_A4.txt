- Acacia: resurrection, immortality, innocence, and continuity of the Mysteries.
- Pine and cone: solar virility, salvific sacrifice, hidden fire.
- Oak, ash, cypress, palm: paternal power, world-axis, maternal principle, positive generative force.
- Grape and wine: false light, ecstasy, solar enthusiasm, temptation.
- Pomegranate: fecundity, underworld bond, concealed abundance, forbidden secret.
- Mandrake and garlic: magical threshold plants, occult potency, protection, and ambiguous animation.

The variety is large, but each example returns to growth, generation, protection, mediation, or transformation.
