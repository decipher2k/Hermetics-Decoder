The dominant law in pass two is unfoldment by stages.

- Seed to plant mirrors potency to manifestation.
- Bud to blossom mirrors chastity to revealed beauty.
- Root to trunk to branch to fruit mirrors source to multiplicity.
- Mud to water to air to light in the lotus mirrors ascent through degrees of consciousness.
- Seasonal death and renewal mirror reincarnation and resurrection.

The chapter therefore presents growth as an initiatory grammar. Plants teach process, not merely emblem.
