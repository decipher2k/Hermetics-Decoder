Final clarification of major symbols:

- Lotus and rose: sacred centers opening toward illumination.
- Flower in general: the reproductive principle purified into beauty and chastity.
- Tree: world-axis, genealogy, body, doctrine, and equilibrium.
- Acacia: resurrection and survival of the hidden life.
- Pine cone and date palm: concentrated generative fire.
- Pomegranate and grape: fecundity, underworld binding, ecstasy, temptation, false light.
- Mandrake and garlic: occult threshold plants, one alluring and ambiguous, the other protective and expulsive.
- Shamrock and wreath: triadic doctrine and consecrated continuity.

The chapter's symbols are strongest when read as sequences of growth, use, and transformation.
