First transmutation:

Plant life becomes the book's most elegant answer to the problem of mediation between matter and spirit. A plant germinates invisibly, rises through graded stages, unfolds form from hidden seed, flowers without violence, fruits through lawful union, and returns cyclically through death and renewal. For that reason it becomes an ideal symbolic medium for speaking of the soul, the cosmos, the Mysteries, and the divine family.

The tree especially gathers several laws at once. It is origin and multiplicity, verticality and rootedness, nourishment and expansion, mortality and recurrence. The flower is a more concentrated version of the same mystery: reproductive force transmuted into beauty and fragrance. The chapter's essential gesture is therefore to purify generation into revelation.
