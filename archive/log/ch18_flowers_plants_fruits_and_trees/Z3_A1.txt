Third pass isolates the chapter's final center: plant life is the exemplary image of lawful manifestation. It shows how invisible potency becomes visible form without rupture. In seeds, blossoms, vines, and trees, the ancient world saw the clearest natural drama of emanation, polarity, maturation, and return.

The chapter's opening controversy over phallic symbolism now appears secondary to this larger claim. Generation is defended because it is the terrestrial analogue of cosmic creativity.
