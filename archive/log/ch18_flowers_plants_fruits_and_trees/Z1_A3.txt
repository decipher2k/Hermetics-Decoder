Primary symbolic clusters in pass one:

- Yonic and phallic vegetal imagery: garden, grail, tower, cone, spear, blossom.
- Lotus and rose: maternal mystery, chakric unfoldment, purity, spiritual awakening.
- Tree symbolism: cosmic axis, genealogy, emanation, rooted nourishment, branching multiplicity, resurrection.
- Sacred woods and species: oak, pine, ash, cypress, palm, acacia.
- Fruits and vines: pomegranate, grape, fig, quince, apricot, apple.
- Magical herbs and plants: mandrake, garlic, onion, shamrock, wreath-bearing evergreens.

The chapter repeatedly treats plants as threshold beings: rooted in earth, yet reaching upward; material, yet quietly luminous with hidden process.
