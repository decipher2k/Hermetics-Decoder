This chapter turns an obscure alchemical manuscript into an exercise in initiatory perception. Hall shows that its many figures do not scatter into unrelated curiosities but repeatedly express the same law of dissolution, gestation, whitening, flowering, and red completion. The raven stage, the child, the queen, the bird, the vessel, and the tree all restage one regenerative drama. Christian names and references provide protective covering, while the deeper structure remains Hermetic and Qabbalistic. The manuscript teaches that true symbolic reading begins when one perceives process persisting beneath changing images.
