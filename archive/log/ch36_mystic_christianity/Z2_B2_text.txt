This chapter interprets Christianity as an initiatory anthropology concealed within sacred story. Hall distinguishes Jesus as bearer and teacher of the mystery from Christos as the universal divine power active within every human being. By reading canonical tensions, apocryphal legends, Essene connections, and comparative savior patterns together, he argues that early Christianity originally carried a doctrine of inner resurrection and reunion rather than mere historical recital. The aim is not to deny Christian meaning but to deepen it by returning it to the universal law of regeneration.
