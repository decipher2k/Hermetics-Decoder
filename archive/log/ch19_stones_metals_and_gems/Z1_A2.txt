First reduction yields six main operations.

1. Stone symbolizes foundation, permanence, and primordial support.
2. Mineral bodies are treated as condensed expressions of divine or celestial powers.
3. Sacred law is repeatedly inscribed in or likened to stone.
4. Pillars, tablets, cubes, and altars make stone architectural and doctrinal at once.
5. Metals and gems correspond to planets and regulate astral influences.
6. Rings and talismans unite form, substance, inscription, and intention in operative symbolism.

The chapter therefore develops a symbolic mineralogy in which durability and receptivity meet.
