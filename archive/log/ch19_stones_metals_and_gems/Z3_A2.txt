Final structural laws:

1. Foundation is sacred because manifestation requires support.
2. Hard matter is especially fit to preserve law and covenant.
3. Polarity becomes intelligible through paired stone forms.
4. Receptivity to force depends on purity, preparation, and correspondence.
5. Craft and inscription are essential to operative symbolism.
6. Wisdom is the true philosopher's stone, transmuting by form and understanding.

These laws make the chapter one of the book's clearest meditations on permanence.
