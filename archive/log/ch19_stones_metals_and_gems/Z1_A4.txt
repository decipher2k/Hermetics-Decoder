First transmutation:

Mineral symbolism answers a question left open by earlier chapters on life, body, and growth: how can spirit persist in form without dissolving into flux? Hall's answer is the stone. Rock, gem, and metal are signs of the point where force has become fixed enough to preserve law, memory, covenant, and power. What plant life teaches through growth, mineral life teaches through endurance.

For that reason the law is written on tablets, temples stand on cornerstones, pillars guard thresholds, rings seal authority, and gems focus planetary influence. The chapter repeatedly presents hard substance as the necessary counterweight to mutability. Revelation requires a medium that can hold.
