Second pass clarifies that the chapter is organized by increasing refinement of fixity. It begins with rough stone and archaic worship, moves through shaped pillars and tablets, rises to precious stones and holy vessels, and ends in carefully combined talismanic forms. The progression is from raw permanence to consciously ordered receptivity.

This makes chapter 19 a strong counterpart to chapter 18. Where plants taught growth and unfoldment, stones teach duration, stabilization, and the retention of power.
