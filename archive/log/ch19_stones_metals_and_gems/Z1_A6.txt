Recomposition sketch:

Hall presents the mineral world as the realm of fixed foundations and condensed powers. Stones symbolize support, endurance, and primordial structure; sacred monoliths, pillars, and altars preserve this logic architecturally and ritually. The tablets of divine law intensify the theme, showing truth impressed into durable medium, while the Grail and sacred gems show matter as the vessel of descending life. Metals and jewels carry the doctrine further by serving as planetary bodies in miniature, each substance partaking of celestial qualities and capable of receiving, regulating, or transmitting them. Rings and talismans complete the chapter by demonstrating that matter, form, inscription, and timing can be deliberately joined for operative ends.

The chapter's inner law is that the enduring body of matter can preserve and mediate spiritual order.
