Final transmutation:

Hall's mineral world is a theology of retention. Stone keeps what time would scatter. Metal conducts what flesh cannot hold steadily. Gem clarifies radiance by purity and concentration. Tablet, pillar, ring, altar, and cup are all versions of one principle: spiritual order requires bodies capable of bearing it. Even the philosopher's stone is finally not a laboratory object but the perfected understanding that can stabilize and transform life.

Thus the chapter turns hardness, weight, brilliance, and form into a grammar of covenant, balance, and preserved power.
