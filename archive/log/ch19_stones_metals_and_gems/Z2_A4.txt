Second-pass clarification of special forms:

- Rock of Saturn: primordial footing and ancestral support.
- Logan stone: poised balance, living stability, dynamic fixity.
- Cube and altar: perfected base supporting fire or spirit.
- Sapphire tables: divine law in luminous purity; rough stone tables: diminished but enduring reflection.
- Grail jewel and cup: fallen radiance, receptive heart, vessel of redeeming life.
- Magical rings and gems: planetary participation through measured material correspondence.

The chapter repeatedly shows that fixed substance is not opposed to vitality. It may be the very condition for reliable manifestation.
