Final clarification of major symbols:

- Stone and bone: the hidden framework beneath embodied life.
- Monolith and rocking stone: archaic sacrality joined to equilibrium.
- Cube and pedestal: perfected support.
- Tablets: divine order fixed in durable form.
- Jachin and Boaz: balanced pillars of active and passive power.
- Grail and sacred jewel: receptive vessel for descending life.
- Metals and gems: crystallized planetary signatures.
- Ring: cyclical completion, sealed authority, embodied equilibrium.

These symbols are strongest when read as media of stabilization, not as dead matter.
