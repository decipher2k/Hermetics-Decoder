Primary symbolic clusters in pass one:

- Stone and bone: support, Saturn, origin, substructure, mortality, permanence.
- Monoliths and rocking stones: archaic worship, balance, living stones, sacred geography.
- Cube, altar, herm, and pillar: stable body supporting active spirit.
- Philosopher's stone: wisdom as transmuting principle.
- Tablets of the Law: divine inscription, dual worlds, masculine and feminine columns, revealed order.
- Holy Grail and sacred stones: vessel, fallen jewel, heart, receptacle of redeeming life.
- Metals and gems: planetary correspondences, talismanic regulation, magical rings.

The whole chapter presses toward one idea: fixed matter can become the bearer of revelation.
