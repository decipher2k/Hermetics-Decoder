The paired pillars now emerge as one of the chapter's strongest keys. Jachin and Boaz, law and prophecy, masculine and feminine, light and darkness, mercy and severity, active and passive force: all these pairings show that mineral symbolism is not merely static. Stone also images polarity held in ordered relation.

The wise man stands between pillars, not on one side of them. This reinforces the larger project's recurring doctrine of equilibrium. Fixed supports are most sacred when they define and sustain a balanced center.
