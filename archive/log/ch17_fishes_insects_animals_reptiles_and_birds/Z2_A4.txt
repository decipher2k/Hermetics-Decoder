Composite creatures now emerge as one of the chapter's strongest keys. They are not failures of observation but intentional symbolic compressions.

The mantichora, sphinx, centaur, harpy, unicorn, man-bull, and other hybrids state that existence is layered and that simple categories cannot contain the soul. These figures disclose thresholds between human, animal, divine, and cosmic states. They dramatize the unfinished, contested, or initiatory character of consciousness.

This means the chapter is also a theory of mythic pedagogy. Hybrid forms arrest literal perception and force the reader into symbolic mode. Their apparent monstrosity is a teaching device that prevents superficial realism.
