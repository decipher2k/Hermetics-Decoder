Final transmutation:

Hall's menagerie is an initiation manual in dispersed form. Each creature teaches how power appears when shaped by a particular medium. Water teaches generation, wing teaches ascent, coil teaches continuity and potency, sting teaches misdirected fire, hive teaches ordered collectivity, mane teaches sovereign radiance, horn teaches penetrative spiritual perception, and ashes teach renewal. The chapter implies that man cannot understand himself without first learning to read these simpler dramatizations of the same forces that move within him.

Thus symbolic zoology becomes practical anthropology. Sacred animals are not remnants of primitive confusion but condensed lessons in how life may be governed, perverted, disciplined, sacrificed, or reborn.
