What must be avoided in later passes:

- Reducing the chapter to antiquarian oddities.
- Treating the creature lists as disconnected encyclopedia entries.
- Flattening symbols into one fixed meaning each.
- Missing the strong continuity with earlier chapters on body, number, initiation, and harmony.

What must be preserved:

- Nature as symbolic script.
- Ambivalence of force.
- Composite creatures as diagrams of layered consciousness.
- Constant transfer between cosmos, ritual, body, and soul.
- Regeneration as the hidden end behind many images, especially serpent, butterfly, scarab, phoenix, lamb, and eagle.
