Final clarification of major symbols:

- Fish: life hidden in immersion, the savior in the deep, initiation through engulfment and return.
- Scarab and butterfly: regeneration by transformation of state.
- Scorpion: the dangerous threshold where fire betrays or illumines.
- Serpent: universal force, wisdom, healing, cyclic immortality, and the awakened spinal current.
- Eagle and phoenix: solar sovereignty transmuted into victorious rebirth.
- Bull and lamb: divine life embodied in sacrificial or seasonal form.
- Dog and horse: fidelity and the disciplined carrying of a greater principle.
- Unicorn and sphinx: spiritual cognition and the guarded threshold of mystery.

These symbols are strongest when treated as processes, not as static correspondences.
