Primary symbolic clusters identified in pass one:

- Fish: origin in water, prolific generation, savior concealed in life, passage through ignorance, redemptive descent.
- Insects: atmospheric agencies, plague, gossip, venom, metamorphosis, labor, sun-symbolism in the scarab, betrayal in the scorpion, soul-flight in the butterfly, communal intelligence in the bee.
- Reptiles: spinal fire, immortality, cyclical renewal, wisdom through temptation, cosmic force, polarity, hidden sight, underworld and healing power.
- Birds: aspiration, mediation between worlds, revelation, solar rulership, incorruptibility, sacrificial love, divine speech, resurrection.
- Animals: solar kingship, fertility, sacrificial embodiment, instinctual burden, faithful service, irrational degeneration, protective ferocity, initiatory strength.

Composite beings unify the chapter. Sphinx, centaur, harpy, mantichora, unicorn, and man-bull all state that visible life can be recombined to show structures of consciousness more clearly than literal anatomy allows.
