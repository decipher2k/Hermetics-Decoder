The dominant law in pass two is transposition. The same symbol is repeatedly moved across domains without losing coherence.

- The fish is biological, initiatory, and christological.
- The scarab is entomological, solar, funerary, and regenerative.
- The scorpion is betrayal, destructive fire, guarded threshold, and wisdom.
- The serpent is temptation, medicine, cyclicity, cosmic electricity, and eternal return.
- The phoenix is bird, sun, initiate, alchemical product, and national emblem.
- The bull is fertility, cosmic season, sacrificial body, and divine incarnation.

This confirms that Hall's symbolic method assumes structural identity across levels of reality. A symbol survives transposition because it names a relation, not an isolated object.
