Third pass isolates the chapter's final center: the world of creatures is treated as a preparatory revelation, a distributed theology written before and beneath formal doctrine. The ancients learn from animals because creation itself is already expressive.

This final pass also shows that the sequence of symbols is not random accumulation. The chapter moves through modes of embodiment, beginning in generative immersion, passing through swarming and coiling agencies, rising into winged transcendence, and ending in the great mammalian and hybrid mirrors of human civilization. The movement is from elemental life toward reflective symbolic likeness.
