Recomposition sketch:

Hall surveys the lower kingdoms of life to show that ancient wisdom traditions read all Nature as revelation. Creaturely form becomes a vocabulary by which invisible agencies are made visible. Fish represent generation and salvific emergence from the waters of life. Insects reveal subtle agencies ranging from corruption and plague to metamorphosis, solar regeneration, and communal labor. Reptiles, above all the serpent, express the dual potential of force itself: wisdom and temptation, immortality and danger, healing and destruction. Birds signify ascent, inspiration, and the soul's movement toward light, culminating in the phoenix as the emblem of rebirth through fire. Mammals and composite beasts articulate sovereignty, sacrifice, fidelity, burden, irrationality, and the mixed constitution of man.

The chapter's real point is methodological: symbolic knowledge studies behavior, form, and relation until the visible world becomes legible as a hierarchy of powers.
