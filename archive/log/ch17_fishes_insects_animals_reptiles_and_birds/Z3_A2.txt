Final structural laws:

1. The visible is pedagogical.
2. Symbolic meaning resides in relation, not label.
3. Force is morally indeterminate until ordered.
4. Regeneration is repeatedly imaged through natural process: shedding, metamorphosis, flight, rebirth from ashes, seasonal renewal.
5. Human consciousness is composite and therefore best taught through composite creatures.
6. Ancient religion is inseparable from close reading of Nature.

These laws make the chapter one of the book's clearest statements that esotericism is observational as well as speculative.
