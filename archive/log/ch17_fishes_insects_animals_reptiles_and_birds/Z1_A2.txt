The first reduction shows five recurrent operations.

1. External form becomes doctrinal shorthand. Habit, habitat, shape, and motion are treated as direct signatures of hidden law.
2. Each creature mediates between scales. A fish may mean germination, baptism, cosmic descent, or Christic preservation without contradiction.
3. Symbolism is dynamic rather than static. The same form can heal or destroy depending on the state of the force it bears.
4. Composite monsters are synthetic diagrams, not failed natural history.
5. Human destiny is continuously reflected in subhuman life. The chapter reads Nature as a set of prehuman mirrors for understanding man.

The chapter thus belongs with the book's strongest methodological sections. It teaches that symbolic interpretation proceeds by relation, function, and energetic analogy, not by arbitrary emblem assignment.
