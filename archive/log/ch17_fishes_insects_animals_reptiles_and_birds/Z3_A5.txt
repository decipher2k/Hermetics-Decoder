What remains indispensable in the final synthesis:

- Keep the chapter unified by its doctrine of Nature as hieroglyphic.
- Preserve the ambivalence of symbols, especially serpent and scorpion.
- Show that rebirth is the hidden culmination of many creature-images.
- Emphasize that hybrid creatures teach the layered constitution of man.
- Link the chapter to the wider project: body, initiation, harmony, and cosmic law all continue here through natural emblems.
