The transcendental postulate deserves special attention. Hall includes the theory that true Rosicrucians operate on inner planes, can function in subtler bodies, and dwell in an etheric temple inaccessible to ordinary sight. Whether accepted literally or not, this hypothesis gives conceptual form to the Brotherhood's invisibility. It says that the fraternity survives because it belongs partly to another order of manifestation.

Even read symbolically, the point is powerful. It means that real initiation changes the mode of being of the initiate, not merely his social affiliation. The Brotherhood is hidden because the unregenerated cannot perceive its level of operation.

This is consistent with the manifestoes' language about clouds, invisibility, and strength borrowed from the eagle. Hall uses the theory to preserve a serious possibility: esoteric societies may have ontological as well as institutional forms.
