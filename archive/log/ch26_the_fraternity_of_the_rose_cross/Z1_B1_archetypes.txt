Core archetypes emerging in the first cycle:

1. The hidden brotherhood.
2. Wisdom buried in legend and recoverable by readiness.
3. The rose-cross as embodied regeneration.
4. Secrecy as protection and test.
5. True lineage concealed within false claimants.
6. Transformative alchemy as inner rather than metallic.
7. Esoteric continuity passing through symbols and orders.
