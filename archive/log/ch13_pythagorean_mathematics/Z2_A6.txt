Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter treats completion as recollection rather than mere endpoint.
- Reduction to 1 or gathering in 10 expresses return as well as origin.
- Numerical process is cyclical.

Operative qualities
Return, recollection, cyclical completion, gathered multiplicity, and closure without loss.

Reformulation

Another strong line in the chapter is the cyclicality of number. Higher quantities are reduced back to foundational digits. The decad gathers all arithmetic and then returns reckoning to the monad. Completion is not termination alone; it is recollection.

This gives the Pythagorean field a return-structure consistent with the book's larger metaphysical movement. Multiplicity expands from principle, only to be re-gathered into intelligible unity.

Even the numerical operations themselves teach this rhythm. Division, halving, summation, and reduction are all exercises in following procession and return.

The chapter thus presents arithmetic as a school of cyclical understanding.
