Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Relational Being
Number discloses how parts belong within wholes and wholes unfold through parts.

2. Lawful Remnancy
Fragments of a lost science still preserve exact directional force.

3. Ontological Typology
Numerical classes express different styles of sufficiency, division, balance, and excess.

4. Developmental Sequence
The first ten numbers stage an ordered unfolding of world-structure.

5. Symbolic Formality
Shape, name, and operation mediate numerical meaning without diluting rigor.

6. Cyclical Recollection
Numerical process returns multiplicity to gathered intelligibility.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle strengthens the whole book's correspondential method by making number the clearest example of lawful procession and return: a field where metaphysics, ethics, symbol, and formal precision remain inseparable.
