Extraction

Central statements
- Mathematical form mediates between abstraction and symbol.
- Figures, solids, and operations carry both technical and imaginal force.
- Number thinks through shape and name.

Operative qualities
Mediation, formal symbolism, imaginal exactness, and articulated abstraction.

Reformulation

The chapter continually mediates between abstract number and symbolic image. Triangles, tetractys, cubes, dodecahedra, sieves, and tables are not embellishments. They are forms through which numerical meaning becomes graspable.

This mediation matters because the Pythagorean science refuses a split between formal exactness and symbolic force. Shape is not external to number. It is one of number's modes of manifestation.

Names of gods, virtues, and cosmic functions likewise do not replace mathematics. They translate its operative character into imaginal terms.

The chapter therefore holds abstraction and symbol together with unusual discipline.
