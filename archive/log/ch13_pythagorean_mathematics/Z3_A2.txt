Extraction

Central statements
- The monad and decad together structure the chapter's full arc.
- One is source; ten is gathered completion.
- The system moves from seed to total articulation and back.

Operative qualities
Arc-structure, source and completion, seed-form, gathered totality, and ordered return.

Reformulation

The chapter's architecture is now especially clear through the relation of monad and decad. It begins from the one as source, proceeds through the differentiated powers of number, and culminates in ten as gathered completion. The whole field of arithmetic lies between seed and total articulation.

This gives the chapter a clean metaphysical arc. Number is born from concentration, unfolds through relation, and returns through recollected fullness.

The table of the ten numbers should thus be read less as a list than as the final expression of the chapter's whole movement.

The science of numbers becomes a science of emergence and return.
