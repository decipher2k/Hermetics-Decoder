Extraction

Central statements
- The table of ten numbers condenses cosmogenesis into sequence.
- The numbers are developmental, not merely enumerative.
- Each numeral stages a movement or condition in unfolding order.

Operative qualities
Sequential emergence, developmental pattern, staged unfolding, and cosmogenic progression.

Reformulation

The table of the ten numbers becomes especially clear when read developmentally. Monad, duad, triad, tetrad, and the later numbers are not just counted items. They stage an unfolding. Unity gives way to polarity, polarity to equilibrated emergence, emergence to stable formation, and so on through vitality, harmony, governance, limitation, and completion.

This developmental sequence turns arithmetic into cosmogenesis. The numbers are not adjacent only by count, but by deepening articulation of the world-process.

Their many divine names should therefore be read as attempts to express each stage's characteristic mode of force.

The chapter thus offers one of the book's clearest examples of sequence as metaphysical drama.
