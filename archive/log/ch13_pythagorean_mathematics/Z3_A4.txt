Extraction

Central statements
- Divine attributions to numbers are functional, not ornamental.
- Gods name powers or modes latent in numerical order.
- Mythic language protects and translates structural insight.

Operative qualities
Functional naming, protective myth, translated power, and symbolic personation.

Reformulation

The divine names attached to numbers become more intelligible here if read functionally. The gods do not decorate arithmetic. They personify or protect the operative characters of numerical powers.

When monad, duad, triad, or pentad are linked with particular deities, virtues, or cosmic agencies, mythic language is translating structure into memorable force. It also shields the science from those unable to read it directly.

This reinforces the chapter's esoteric strategy. Symbolic naming both reveals and veils.

The chapter thus shows how mathematical insight may survive by passing into sacred language without surrendering precision.
