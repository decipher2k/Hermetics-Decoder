Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Numerical Source
Number is the first articulation through which hidden order becomes intelligible.

2. Reduction to Principle
Derived forms gain meaning when traced back to linguistic, numerical, and structural origins.

3. Emanative Unity
The many unfold from the monad without losing relation to source.

4. Qualitative Polarity
Numerical classes express modes of difference, complementarity, and mediation.

5. Ethical Measure
Arithmetic images the law of virtue through balance between excess and defect.

6. Decadic Completion
Multiplicity gathers into totality through the powers condensed in the ten numbers.

Relation to the whole work
This first cycle places Pythagorean mathematics at the center of the book's correspondential method by showing that number underlies cosmology, ethics, symbolic language, and the readable structure of the world.
