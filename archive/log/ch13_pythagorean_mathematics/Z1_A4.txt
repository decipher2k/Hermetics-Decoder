Extraction

Central statements
- Odd and even are treated as archetypal polarities rather than bare classifications.
- Masculine/feminine, definite/indefinite, stable/movable are read numerically.
- Unity itself mediates and complicates polarity.

Operative qualities
Polarity, mediation, complementarity, qualitative difference, and structured opposition.

Reformulation

The chapter also treats numerical classes as qualitative modes. Odd and even are not merely counting categories. They become figures of stability and indefiniteness, masculine and feminine, center and divisible spread. Yet unity refuses to remain simple within this polarity, because it can generate either side.

This complication matters. The Pythagorean field is not dualism in a crude sense. It is an articulated polarity stabilized by a principle that can mediate both extremes.

The long classifications of incomposite and composite numbers, evenly-even and evenly-odd, superabundant, deficient, and perfect, all extend this same impulse: number is a language for distinguishing different modes of relation and proportion.

The chapter therefore converts arithmetic into a qualitative science of ordered differences.
