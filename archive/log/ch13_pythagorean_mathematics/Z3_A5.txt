Central statements
- Number in this chapter governs not only cosmos but interpretation itself.
- The reader is trained to think in terms of proportion, sequence, and centered relation.

Operative qualities
Interpretive formation, proportional reading, sequenced thought, and centered intelligibility.

Reformulation

By this point the chapter is shaping not only what is known but how one reads. The reader is taught to search for sequence, centrality, proportion, complementarity, and return. Number becomes a hermeneutic discipline.

This matters for the larger book because it provides a method transferable beyond arithmetic. Symbolic objects, myths, bodies, rites, and cosmologies can all be approached numerically in the sense that their relations are primary.

The chapter therefore does double work. It presents a doctrine of number and simultaneously trains a mode of interpretation.

Pythagorean mathematics becomes a school of ordered reading.
