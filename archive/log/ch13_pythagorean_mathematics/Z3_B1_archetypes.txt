Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Exact Symbolic Intelligence
Number joins formal rigor to metaphysical and symbolic depth.

2. Source-to-Completion Arc
The numerical field unfolds from monadic seed to decadic totality and recollects itself.

3. Contemplative Classification
Technical distinctions train attention into ordered seeing.

4. Protective Naming
Divine and mythic attributions translate structural powers without surrendering secrecy.

5. Hermeneutic Proportion
Number teaches a transferable method of reading by relation, sequence, and center.

6. Principled Esotericism
True hidden knowledge remains exact and resists both flattening and fantasy.

Relation to the whole work
This final cycle makes Pythagorean mathematics one of the book's strongest methodological chapters: it not only explains a symbolic science of number but also trains the reader in the disciplined correspondential reading required by the work as a whole.
