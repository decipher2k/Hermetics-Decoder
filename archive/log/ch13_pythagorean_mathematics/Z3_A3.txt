Extraction

Central statements
- Classification in the chapter functions as disciplined contemplation.
- To sort numbers properly is to train perception in order.
- Technical labor and inward seeing are joined.

Operative qualities
Contemplative classification, trained perception, ordered attention, and technical inwardness.

Reformulation

The chapter's technical detail also reveals a contemplative function. Sorting numbers into proper classes, tracing their ratios, and observing their operations disciplines attention. The intellect learns to dwell in order rather than in confusion.

This means classification is not mere bookkeeping. It becomes a practice of inward seeing. The mind is trained by exact distinctions to recognize the styles of relation that structure reality.

Pythagorean mathematics is therefore both technical and spiritual. Its rigor is itself a mode of contemplation.

The chapter helps explain why formal study could belong to initiation rather than standing outside it.
