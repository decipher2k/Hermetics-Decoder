Extraction

Central statements
- The decad gathers the system into completion.
- The table of the ten numbers turns arithmetic into symbolic cosmology.
- Each numeral is treated as a principial power with many names and correspondences.

Operative qualities
Completion, symbolic concentration, principial naming, and totalized order.

Reformulation

The chapter culminates in the table of the ten numbers, where arithmetic becomes explicit symbolic cosmology. Each numeral is not only defined but named across multiple divine, cosmic, and qualitative registers. Number here becomes a set of living principles.

The decad is especially decisive because it gathers and completes the field. Returning to ten is not a mere counting habit. It signifies the re-collection of multiplicity into intelligible totality.

This explains why the chapter dwells so heavily on the symbolic character of monad, duad, triad, tetrad, and the rest. The numbers are read as modes through which the world is generated, differentiated, reconciled, vitalized, governed, and brought toward completion.

The chapter's later catalogue should therefore be read as concentrated ontology rather than decorative numerology.
