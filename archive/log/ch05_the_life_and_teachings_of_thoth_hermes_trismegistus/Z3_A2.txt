Extraction

Central statements
- The books, rites, and sciences are expressions of one preserving current.
- Hermetic tradition is the continuity-form of insight.

Operative qualities
Preserving current, continuity-form, ritual inscription, and civilizational memory.

Reformulation

The chapter treats books, rites, symbols, and sciences as expressions of one preserving current. Hermetic tradition is the continuity-form of insight. It carries what could otherwise be lost through burning, fragmentation, or forgetfulness.

This makes the surviving fragments doubly meaningful: incomplete as documents, yet still charged as carriers of pattern.
