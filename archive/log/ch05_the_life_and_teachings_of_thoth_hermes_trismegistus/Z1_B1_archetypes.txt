Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Universal Mediation
Hermes unites cosmic intelligence and historical instruction.

2. Luminous Discernment
The central human task is distinguishing true self from reflected and composite identity.

3. Guarded Continuity
Wisdom survives through books, rites, symbols, and initiatory succession.

4. Ordered Ascent
Return to source occurs through lawful stripping of lower accretions.

5. Consecrated Speech
Reason becomes salvific when voiced by awakened intelligence.

6. Illuminative Service
Revelation fulfills itself by becoming guidance for others.

Relation to the whole work
This chapter sharpens the whole book's claim that hidden wisdom is not random antiquarian residue but an organized current of transmission joining cosmology, anthropology, ritual, and moral transformation.
