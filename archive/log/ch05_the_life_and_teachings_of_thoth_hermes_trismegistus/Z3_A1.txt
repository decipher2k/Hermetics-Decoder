Central statements

Operative qualities

Reformulation

The chapter's final center is Hermetic mediation as lived intelligence. Hermes is not important only because he reveals truth, but because he embodies a relation to truth: lucid, ordered, reverent, active, and responsible. He becomes the visible form of awakened participation in universal mind.
