Z2_A6

Extraction

Central statements
- Hermes as teacher embodies the conversion of revelation into method.
- Mind protects the worthy by becoming an inner gatekeeper.

Operative qualities
Method, interior protection, worthiness, selective help, and guided awakening.

Reformulation

Hermes is not only the seer of truth but the converter of revelation into method. The chapter insists that mind comes to the holy, pure, and merciful, guards them against lower impulses, and becomes a living gatekeeper. This makes wisdom selective without reducing it to exclusion. Capacity matters because the true obstacle lies in the unpurified lower nature.

Guidance is therefore cooperative: revelation meets preparedness.
