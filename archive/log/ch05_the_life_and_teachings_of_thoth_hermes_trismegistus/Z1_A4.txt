Extraction

Central statements
- The seven planetary governors structure mortality and ascent.
- Destiny binds the lower being, but does not erase the higher possibility.
- Salvation is an ordered stripping away.

Operative qualities
Planetary law, destiny, purification, relinquishment, and ascending release.

Reformulation

The chapter gives mortality a precise structure through the seven governors. The lower life is governed by patterned forces: increase and decrease, deceit, passion, ambition, rashness, acquisitiveness, and falsity. These are not merely moral faults. They are the binding powers of mixed existence.

Yet destiny is not ultimate. The same ordered cosmos that binds the lower composite being also supplies the ladder of return. At death and in spiritual awakening the soul rises through the rings, restoring to each sphere the powers that had held it.

Salvation in this chapter is therefore not an instantaneous pardon but an ordered relinquishment. One becomes free by giving back what does not belong to the true self.
