Z3_A5

Extraction

Central statements
- The ascent through the spheres is the practical anatomy of freedom.
- Freedom means release from borrowed governance.

Operative qualities
Practical freedom, unbinding, anatomical ascent, and recovered sovereignty.

Reformulation

The ascent through the spheres functions as a practical anatomy of freedom. The soul becomes free not by gaining a new arbitrary independence, but by being released from borrowed governance. Each sphere receives back one layer of lower determination until the luminous core stands unencumbered.

The chapter's freedom is therefore ordered and intelligible, not merely emotional.
