Central statements
- Poimandres yields the chapter's ontological hierarchy.

Operative qualities
Ontological hierarchy, luminous priority, rational articulation, and mixed embodiment.

Reformulation

Poimandres establishes the chapter's ontological hierarchy. Mind, light, and archetype are primary. Reason articulates and orders. Matter, darkness, and mixed embodiment are secondary states that derive their structure from higher causes.

The human task follows from that hierarchy: live according to the higher principle rather than remain fascinated by derivative conditions.
