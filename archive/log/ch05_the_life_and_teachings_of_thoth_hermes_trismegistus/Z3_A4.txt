Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter's fall doctrine is now fully centered on misrelation.
- Mortality is intensified by attachment to the lower image.

Operative qualities
Misrelation, attachment, derivative identity, and restorative discrimination.

Reformulation

The chapter's doctrine of fall is best described as misrelation. Mortality intensifies when consciousness attaches itself to the lower image and begins to live from what is derivative rather than from what is primary. Redemption is therefore restorative discrimination: returning relation to its proper order.
