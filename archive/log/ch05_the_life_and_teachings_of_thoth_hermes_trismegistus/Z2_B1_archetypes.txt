Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Transmissible Mind
Universal intelligence becomes operative in history through disciplined mediators.

2. Patterned Reality
The world is intelligible because archetypal order precedes manifestation.

3. Reflective Bondage
The soul's fall is fascination with likeness and shadow.

4. Liberating Relinquishment
Ascent occurs by returning borrowed distortions to the spheres that govern them.

5. Selective Illumination
Mind aids those whose inward condition can receive it.

6. Methodical Service
Revelation fulfills itself by becoming a repeatable path for others.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle integrates Hermes more tightly into the book's larger method: symbolic forms, sacred sciences, and initiatory institutions all derive their force from a prior intelligible order that trained mediators can transmit without fully exhausting.
