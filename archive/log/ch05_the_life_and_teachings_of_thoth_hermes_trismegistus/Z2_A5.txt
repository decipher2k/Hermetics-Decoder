Z2_A5

Extraction

Central statements
- The planetary ascent is a disciplined deconstruction of the false self.
- Each ring marks a relinquished distortion.

Operative qualities
Disciplined deconstruction, relinquishment, purification, and return through subtraction.

Reformulation

The ascent through the seven rings is the disciplined deconstruction of the false self. Each sphere receives back a distortion or attachment: fluctuation, deceit, passion, ambition, violence, accumulation, falsity. The ascent is therefore not accumulation of powers but subtraction of what never truly belonged to the immortal principle.

This gives the chapter one of its most exact spiritual laws: return happens by relinquishment.
