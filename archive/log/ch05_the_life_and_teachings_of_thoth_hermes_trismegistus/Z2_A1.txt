Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter is best read as a theology of mediation.
- Hermes is the point where universal mind becomes transmissible instruction.

Operative qualities
Mediation, transmissibility, sacred instruction, and luminous authority.

Reformulation

The chapter increasingly reads as a theology of mediation. Hermes matters because he makes the universal mind transmissible. He is the form through which hidden intelligence becomes instruction, ritual continuity, symbolic language, and practical ascent.

This mediatory role explains the chapter's combination of legend, book tradition, cosmology, and mission. All are expressions of one function: to carry what is higher into forms that can guide human transformation.
