Z2_A3

Extraction

Central statements
- Poimandres is the chapter's governing revelation of archetypal reality.
- The visible world is cast from prior pattern.

Operative qualities
Archetypal priority, visionary disclosure, pattern before form, and metaphysical intelligibility.

Reformulation

Poimandres provides the governing revelation: reality is patterned before it appears. Light, mind, archetype, and word precede visible formation. The world is intelligible because it is cast from prior pattern, not because meaning is later imposed upon it.

This makes vision epistemically central. Hermes knows because he is raised into the level where pattern is seen before confusion hardens into ordinary perception.
