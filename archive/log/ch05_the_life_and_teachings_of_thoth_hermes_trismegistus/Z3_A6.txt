Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter's ethics are inseparable from its metaphysics.
- Purity, mercy, piety, and service are conditions of reception.

Operative qualities
Ethical receptivity, purity, mercy, piety, and transmissive obligation.

Reformulation

The chapter binds ethics tightly to metaphysics. Mind comes to the holy, good, pure, and merciful. This means that reception of truth depends on inward condition. Knowledge here is inseparable from purification, and insight obligates service.

Hermes is exemplary not only because he sees, but because he becomes trustworthy to what he has seen.
