Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Mediated Awakening
Universal mind reaches human beings through forms of disciplined mediation.

2. Luminous Priority
Higher order, not lower mixture, defines the truth of the self.

3. Reflective Error
Bondage begins when likeness is mistaken for reality.

4. Ordered Freedom
Release comes by surrendering borrowed determinations and returning to source.

5. Ethical Receptivity
Purity and mercy are not moral ornaments but conditions of illumination.

6. Faithful Continuance
Wisdom remains active in history when revelation becomes teachable, guardable, and repeatable.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle fixes Hermes as one of the book's master figures of transmission: a model for how hidden law enters symbol, scripture, rite, science, and living discipline without losing its origin in a higher intelligible order.
