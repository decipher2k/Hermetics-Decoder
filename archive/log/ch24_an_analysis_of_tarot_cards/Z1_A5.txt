What must be avoided:

- Treating the chapter as fortune-telling lore.
- Reducing the card descriptions to disconnected iconographic notes.
- Missing the structural link between Tarot, Qabbalah, alchemy, Masonry, and anthropology.
- Ignoring the chapter's strong argument about concealment through popular use.

What must be preserved:

- Tarot as symbolic book.
- Each card as both principle and position in sequence.
- The zero card as threshold and mystery.
- Minor cards as surviving calendrical and cosmological code.
