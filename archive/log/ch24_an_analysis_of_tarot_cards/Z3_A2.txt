Final structural laws:

1. Wisdom may be preserved by being mistaken for play.
2. Images can function as exact symbolic propositions.
3. Sequence is part of meaning, not an afterthought.
4. The threshold mystery precedes enumeration and animates it.
5. Reduced popular forms can still carry intact structural traces.
6. Gaming systems can miniature cosmic, calendrical, and anthropological orders.

These laws make the chapter one of the book's clearest reflections on symbolic transmission.
