1. Sacred knowledge can survive by disguising itself as play.
2. Symbolic images operate as both isolated teachings and members of a larger sequence.
3. Number, order, and pictorial form must be read together.
4. The major trumps map the stages and powers of the human and cosmic process.
5. The deck as a whole mirrors calendrical, elemental, social, and metaphysical structures.
6. Popular gaming culture can unknowingly preserve initiatory doctrine.

The chapter is therefore a theory of encrypted symbolic transmission.
