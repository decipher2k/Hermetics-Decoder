Primary symbolic clusters in pass one:

- Origins of Tarot in Egyptian, Arabic, Templar, Gypsy, Rosicrucian, and Masonic currents.
- Tarot as book, road, alphabet, and universal key.
- Controversies over sequence, Hebrew correspondences, and the zero card.
- Major trumps as archetypal stations: magician, priestess, empress, emperor, hierophant, lovers, chariot, justice, hermit, wheel, strength, hanged man, death, temperance, devil, tower, stars, moon, sun, judgment, world.
- Minor arcana as elemental, social, calendrical, and Qabbalistic pattern.
- Playing cards and chess as surviving fragments of the same symbolic logic.

These clusters unify around one thesis: the world of games can be a masked school of wisdom.
