First transmutation:

The chapter makes secrecy dynamic rather than static. Wisdom is not only hidden by concealment but by misclassification. Once the symbolic book is judged to be merely a pastime, its contents become safe from persecution and safe for transmission. Play becomes a veil stronger than locks.

The Tarot's deeper force lies in seriality. No card is fully intelligible alone because each represents a phase or mode within a moving totality. The fool's entry, the magician's skill, the priestess's concealment, the lovers' choice, the hanged reversal, the tower's rupture, the world's completion: these are not random pictures but ordered transformations. The chapter teaches that symbolic decks can narrate process without explicit prose.
