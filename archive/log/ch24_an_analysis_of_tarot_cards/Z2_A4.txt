Second-pass clarification of special forms:

- Major trumps: initiatory stations and cosmic laws.
- Zero card: threshold, unreality, latent totality, or disguised source.
- Minor suits: elemental and social divisions, also temporal cycles.
- Court cards: zodiacal, dynastic, or hierarchical embodiments.
- Playing-card reductions: broken but surviving symbolic calendar.
- Chess analogy: game structure as cosmological and anthropological drama.

These forms show that symbolic gaming systems can carry multiple scales of meaning at once.
