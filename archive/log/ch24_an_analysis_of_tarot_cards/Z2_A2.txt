The dominant law in pass two is sequential metamorphosis.

- The fool enters the path.
- Skill, law, authority, and polarity frame the field.
- Choice, movement, justice, and withdrawal shape the journey.
- Reversal, death, and tempering transform the seeker.
- Temptation, destruction, and uncertainty purify perception.
- Illumination, awakening, and the world-crown complete the cycle.

The chapter thus shows that the deck is not only symbolic inventory but dramatic progression.
