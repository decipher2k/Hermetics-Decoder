The zero card now stands out as the chapter's strongest interpretive problem and one of its deepest clues. Its unstable placement reveals that the deck begins in mystery before enumeration. The unnumbered threshold precedes count yet enters count; it is both outside the series and the condition for traversing it.

This makes the Fool less a comic figure than a profound emblem of entry into manifested experience, illusion, risk, and hidden possibility. The chapter's logic of encrypted wisdom is concentrated there.
