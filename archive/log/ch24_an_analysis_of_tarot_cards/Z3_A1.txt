Third pass isolates the chapter's final center: the Tarot is a machine of symbolic memory. It stores a universal doctrine in portable, memorable, re-orderable image units. Because it can be handled, shuffled, played, and taught without open proclamation, it becomes an unusually durable vessel for esoteric transmission.

This makes chapter 24 a major complement to the Qabbalah chapter. Where Qabbalah emphasizes letters and numbers, Tarot emphasizes image and sequence, but both are architectonic symbolic languages.
