Recomposition sketch:

Hall presents the Tarot as a concealed philosophical text whose images preserve universal laws, principles, and stages of the initiatory process. The cards are at once separate hieroglyphs and components of a symbolic alphabet, and their meanings emerge through both individual iconography and ordered sequence. The major trumps portray the powers and transitions of the human and cosmic drama, from ignorance and entrance through choice, ordeal, sacrifice, death, balance, illumination, judgment, and completion. The minor arcana extend this logic into the elements, the social orders, the calendar, the worlds of Qabbalah, and the rhythms of time.

The chapter's inner law is that wisdom can survive by passing into the form of play.
