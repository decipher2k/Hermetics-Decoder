Final transmutation:

Hall's Tarot is a hidden curriculum in image form. It demonstrates how an entire philosophy can be compressed into a deck whose surfaces appear recreational while its structure preserves cosmology, anthropology, initiation, and time. Each card is memorable because it is pictorial, but it becomes intelligible only when number, posture, emblem, and place in sequence are read together. The deck is thus not commentary on wisdom but one of wisdom's practical media.

The chapter also shows that symbolic systems survive by adaptation. Even when court cards change, pages vanish, or decks become simplified for gambling, traces of the original architectonic remain. Knowledge can decay and still persist.
