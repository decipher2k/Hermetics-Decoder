Final clarification of major symbols:

- Tarot as book, road, and alphabet.
- Fool: masked beginning, unreality, latent totality, and initiatory entry.
- Major trumps: ordered transformations of consciousness and cosmos.
- Minor trumps: fourfold world distributed into time, class, and element.
- Playing cards and chess: surviving fragments of older symbolic grammars.

These symbols are strongest when read as a portable architecture of wisdom.
