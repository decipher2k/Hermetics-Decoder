The dominant law in pass two is specialization.

- Gnomes work with solidity, roots, metals, bones, and hidden treasure.
- Undines work with liquidity, beauty, vital essences, and flow.
- Salamanders work with heat, combustion, temperament, blood, and intensity.
- Sylphs work with gases, clouds, inspiration, changeability, and nervous subtlety.

The chapter thus uses personification to map differentiated process. Each kingdom is a mode of intelligence tied to one domain of becoming.
