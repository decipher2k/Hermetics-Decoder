Recomposition sketch:

Hall, drawing on Paracelsus, presents the four elements as each possessing both visible substance and invisible etheric essence. Within these subtler elemental bodies dwell the Nature spirits: gnomes in earth, undines in water, salamanders in fire, and sylphs in air. These beings are neither immortal spirits nor ordinary animals, but intermediate organisms constituted of one elemental principle and therefore expert within it. They personify the hidden agencies behind minerals, plants, heat, weather, fluids, and atmospheric life.

The chapter also carefully distinguishes these spirits from demons, larvæ, incubi, succubi, and vampires. Its deeper claim is that the invisible world is internally differentiated, and that Nature is full of lawful but subtle grades of life.
