Primary symbolic clusters in pass one:

- Dual constitution of elements: visible body and invisible essence.
- Intermediate beings: composita between matter and spirit.
- Four elemental families: gnomes, undines, salamanders, sylphs.
- Specialized domains: minerals and roots, fluids and beauty, heat and passion, gases and inspiration.
- Elemental rulers, directions, temperaments, and zodiacal assignments.
- Distinction among elementals, demons, larvæ, vampires, and higher intelligences.

These clusters unify around one thesis: Nature is organized by living subtleties, not mechanical lifelessness alone.
