First transmutation:

The chapter restores animation to the cosmos without collapsing everything into abstract spirit. Its beings are specific, local, functional, and bounded. Each elemental order inhabits one kind of subtle medium and becomes expert in that domain. Hence the world is not only alive but differentiated. Intelligence here is distributed across levels, functions, and substances.

The importance of this chapter lies in method as much as content. It gives symbolic form to natural process. Mineral crystallization, plant selection, heat, storm, inspiration, fluidity, and atmospheric change are interpreted as the operations of subtler workers. Whether read literally or analogically, the chapter insists that natural process expresses purposive pattern.
