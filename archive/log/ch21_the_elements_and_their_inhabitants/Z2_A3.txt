The human being now stands out as a crucial hinge. Elementals are one-sided, composed of one elemental ether, but man contains centers responsive to all four. This makes him less specialized yet more comprehensive. The chapter therefore quietly deepens the book's anthropology: man is a mediator because his constitution is composite.

This also explains the ambiguity of contact. Human beings can commune with these forces, but they can also misread, exploit, or fear them, especially when ecclesiastical simplification labels them all demons.
