Final clarification of major symbols:

- Four elementals: living modalities of earth, water, fire, and air.
- Elemental rulers, directions, and temperaments: the ordering of subtle domains.
- Gnome, undine, salamander, sylph: solidity, flow, heat, and inspiration personified.
- Larvæ and vampire forms: degraded astral products distinct from elemental orders.
- Human contact: composite consciousness touching specialized worlds.

These symbols are strongest when read as a taxonomy of living process.
