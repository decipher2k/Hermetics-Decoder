Final transmutation:

Hall's occult pneumatology is a science of the middle realms. It neither abandons Nature to blind mechanism nor dissolves it into a vague spirituality. Instead it proposes that every visible element is underlaid by an invisible medium inhabited by localized intelligences whose work gives form, continuity, and style to the processes we observe. What looks merely like growth, flow, fire, weather, or inspiration becomes legible as differentiated activity.

The chapter therefore turns fairy lore into metaphysics. The point is not fantasy for its own sake, but the claim that life and intelligence are more finely distributed through the cosmos than ordinary perception admits.
