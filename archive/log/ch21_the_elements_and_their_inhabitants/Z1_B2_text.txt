This chapter presents the elements as double in constitution and Nature as inhabited at every level. Earth, water, fire, and air each possess not only a visible body but also a subtler etheric essence. Within these elemental ethers dwell the Nature spirits, intermediate beings who stand between gross matter and higher spiritual hierarchies.

Gnomes, undines, salamanders, and sylphs are not treated as decorative myths but as specialized intelligences working through distinct natural domains. They govern minerals, roots, fluids, weather, heat, gases, beauty, and inspiration according to the nature of the element through which they function. Because man contains relations to all four elemental fields, limited contact between human consciousness and these beings is possible.

The chapter's deeper purpose is to restore lawful animation to the cosmos. Invisible life is differentiated, not chaotic, and should not be confused either with evil entities or with the higher orders of angels and divine beings. Nature is shown as a world of subtle workers and layered realities.
