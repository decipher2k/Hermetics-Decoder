What must be avoided:

- Treating the chapter as naive fairy lore detached from the book's symbolic method.
- Collapsing all invisible beings into one undifferentiated category.
- Missing the structural importance of the fourfold elemental scheme.
- Reducing the material to superstition rather than a model of distributed intelligence.

What must be preserved:

- Dual aspect of each element.
- Intermediate ontological status of elementals.
- Functional specialization of each kingdom.
- Distinction between elementals and morally degraded astral entities.
