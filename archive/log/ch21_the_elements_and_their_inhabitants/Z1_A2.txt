First reduction yields six governing operations.

1. Every element has an inner and outer body.
2. Nature is inhabited at every level by specialized consciousness.
3. Elementals are intermediate beings, neither fully material nor fully spiritual.
4. Each class of elemental governs a domain of process and function.
5. Human beings can occasionally contact these kingdoms because all four elemental principles meet in man.
6. Not every invisible being is evil; moral confusion begins when all subtle life is demonized.

The chapter therefore develops a symbolic ecology of the invisible world.
