Second pass clarifies that the chapter is organized by ontological discrimination. Hall is carefully sorting invisible beings into kinds. This matters because confusion at this level produces both superstition and dogmatic rejection. The chapter therefore works by making precise distinctions: elemental versus angelic, elemental versus larval, elemental versus vampiric, intermediate versus immortal.

Its teaching is not that every unseen thing is exalted, but that unseen life is stratified and must be read according to substance, function, and destiny.
