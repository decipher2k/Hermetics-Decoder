Second-pass clarification of special forms:

- Gnomes: secrecy, rooted labor, treasure, structural support.
- Undines: grace, emotion, moisture, beautification, flow.
- Salamanders: energy, danger, combustion, passion, transformative heat.
- Sylphs: inspiration, volatility, imagination, mobility, subtle communication.
- Incubus, succubus, larvæ, vampire: not elemental orders, but degraded astral formations or parasitic remnants.

This distinction is essential: the chapter protects the invisible world from crude moral flattening.
