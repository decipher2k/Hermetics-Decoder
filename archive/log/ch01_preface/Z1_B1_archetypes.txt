Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Worthy Vessel
Ancient insight demands an embodiment proportionate to its dignity. Form is not decorative surplus; it is part of truthful transmission.

2. Hidden Labor Becoming Visible
What appears as a finished book is the surfaced edge of years of gathering, translation, revision, and arrangement.

3. Humble Fidelity
The proper stance before fragmentary wisdom is neither skeptical dismissal nor dogmatic ownership, but disciplined responsibility.

4. Non-Coercive Synthesis
Traditions may be placed in relation without being violently forced into sameness.

5. Interior Readiness
Subtle truth requires a reader whose perception is active, patient, and more than literal.

6. Collective Custodianship
Wisdom survives through networks of helpers, institutions, craftsmen, and believers, not through solitary genius alone.

Relation to the whole work
These evolved qualities provide the Preface with its enduring role in the book. It is not merely introductory courtesy. It establishes the ethic of transmission for everything that follows: preserve without flattening, organize without dominating, and receive with reverent intelligence.
