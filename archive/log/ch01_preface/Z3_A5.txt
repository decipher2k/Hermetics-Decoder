Reformulation

This Preface now reveals the book as an act of moral craft. If enduring truth is to be handed forward faithfully, the form that carries it must be made with responsibility. The care given to this volume belongs to that responsibility.

What appears finished here was long in preparation. Years of research, collection, translation, and arrangement preceded the writing itself. Footnotes were removed to preserve continuity of movement. References were placed within the text. Bibliography and index were retained so that the reader might continue the inquiry.

I claim neither infallibility nor detached originality. Ancient sources remain incomplete, and that incompleteness demands interpretive patience. Some statements may challenge expectation, but they have been advanced with care and with loyalty to the inner force of the sources. Where the work fails, I accept responsibility.

I have not used the material to justify a private system. I have not manufactured unity by denying genuine divergence.

The book opposes modern impatience by making room for realities that are not immediately reducible.

Such realities ask for a patient reader.

They also ask us to recognize that this book came into being through many forms of help.

It is therefore a cooperative act of faithful making.

If the reader learns patience from the text as the text itself was made through patience, then the Preface has become fully intelligible.
