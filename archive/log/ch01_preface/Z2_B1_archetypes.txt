Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Transmission as Embodiment
A teaching survives only when it finds a form capable of carrying its weight without collapse.

2. Visible Form from Invisible Preparation
Public expression is the late stage of a long hidden gestation of collecting, translating, arranging, and refining.

3. Responsible Nearness
The interpreter must come close enough to preserve spirit, yet not so close as to claim ownership.

4. Widening Against Reduction
A serious book can oppose the age not by rejecting thought, but by enlarging the field in which thought is allowed to move.

5. Reading as Participation
Understanding subtle material requires active inward cooperation from the reader.

6. Gratitude as Ontology of Work
No durable transmission is solitary; dependence is part of the truth of the finished artifact.

Relation to the whole work
In this second cycle, the Preface becomes even more clearly the work's ethical key. It establishes that wisdom is transmitted through proportionate form, disciplined humility, and shared custodianship.
