Reformulation

The Preface has settled into a clear claim: truth of lasting depth must be given a form that serves it rather than weakens it. That claim governs the making of this book. Its care of arrangement, presentation, and material dignity belongs to the same fidelity by which it handles its subject.

The finished manuscript is the visible end of a much longer invisible preparation. Research, collecting, translating, and ordering came first. Footnotes were removed to preserve continuity. References were woven into the text. Bibliography and index were retained to support continued study.

I do not claim infallibility, because the material itself does not allow such a claim. Ancient teachings survive in fragments, and responsible interpretation must remain conscious of limit. Some passages here may ask much of the reader, yet they have been shaped with restraint and with an effort to preserve inner intention rather than outer shell. If errors remain, they belong to me.

The book does not impose a personal doctrine, nor does it erase real differences by force.

It does oppose reduction. It asks whether modern thought has mistaken its own limits for the limits of reality.

To engage that question, the reader must read symbolically rather than reductively.

The work also acknowledges every form of shared labor through which it became possible.

Its gratitude is therefore structural, not incidental.

If the reader finishes the Preface prepared to meet form as carrier of depth, then the whole book can begin.
