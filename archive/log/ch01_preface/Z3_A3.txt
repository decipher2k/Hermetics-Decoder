Central statements
- The Preface frames the whole project as disciplined remembrance.
- Truth is preserved through care, not through haste or ownership.
- The reader is invited into a widened relation to meaning.

Operative qualities
Remembrance, care, non-ownership, and widened relation.

Reformulation

This book is an act of disciplined remembrance. It proceeds from the conviction that truths capable of surviving centuries should not be entrusted to careless form. Since ancient wisdom has often reached the present in reduced expression, I have tried to offer it a more faithful body, one in which visible care supports rather than weakens invisible meaning.

The years of writing were preceded by years of gathering. Sources had to be found, languages crossed, patterns recognized, and materials arranged before the manuscript could become stable. Footnotes were removed to keep the current of thought unbroken. References were folded into the text itself. Bibliography and index remain as instruments for anyone wishing to continue the work of understanding.

I make no claim of final certainty. Fragmentary evidence does not allow it. Some statements may appear surprising, yet they have been chosen with restraint and with an effort to preserve the original impulse of the material. If mistakes remain, they are mine.

This volume does not force the past into a private creed, nor does it deny genuine difference among traditions.

Its orientation is nevertheless opposed to modern reduction. It seeks to restore a wider relation to reality than contemporary habits usually permit.

For that reason the reader must bring more than literal attention. These subjects require inward participation.

The book also testifies to collective labor. Libraries, translators, editors, artists, printers, and supporters all helped bring it into being.

Its existence is therefore a sign that serious transmission is shared work.

If it teaches the reader to remember hidden relation where only fragments once appeared, then it has justified its making.
