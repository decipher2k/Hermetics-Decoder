Operative qualities

Reformulation

Faithful transmission depends on three recognitions: the material must be handled with care, the interpreter must respect limit, and serious work is never solitary. This Preface establishes all three. It arises from the conviction that the hidden wisdom of the past deserves a form that does not diminish it.

The book was written over years, but writing came after gathering, translation, and preparation. Footnotes were removed to preserve continuity. References were integrated. Bibliography and index were preserved as tools for later readers.

I do not surrender the material to skepticism, yet neither do I seize it with dogmatic certainty. Ancient testimony survives only in part. Some claims here may seem unusual, but they have been chosen with restraint and with a desire to preserve the inward motion of the sources. Whatever mistakes remain are mine.

No private creed controls the use of these materials. No false unity has been manufactured.

The book seeks to reopen symbolic depth within a culture trained to prefer reduction.

For that reason, the reader must become ready for symbolic reading.

The work also remembers the many people and institutions through which it became possible.

Its gratitude acknowledges that truth often survives by shared labor.

If the reader becomes capable of perceiving more than literal statement, then this opening section has already accomplished its aim.
