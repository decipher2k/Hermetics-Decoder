Central statements
- The Preface now emphasizes proportion between invisible content and visible form.
- Interpretation requires honest awareness of limit.
- The book seeks relation rather than fragmentation.
- Gratitude becomes structural rather than personal only.

Operative qualities
Proportion, honest limit, relation, and structural gratitude.

Reformulation

The governing impulse of this book is proportionality. Wisdom of enduring depth should not be entrusted to forms that trivialize it. Because the hidden philosophies of antiquity have often suffered exactly that fate, this work attempts to carry them in a vessel more adequate to their gravity. The visible dignity of the book is therefore not a luxury but part of faithful transmission.

Its pages were written over years, yet the writing rests on prior years of collection, translation, comparison, and ordering. The book had to be prepared inwardly before it could be composed outwardly. Footnotes were set aside to preserve continuity. References were absorbed into the text. A bibliography remains for continued inquiry, and an index offers relation where sheer density might otherwise produce confusion.

I do not claim certainty beyond the fragmentary nature of the evidence. Ancient teachings do not permit that. Some conclusions here may appear daring, but they have not been chosen recklessly. I have tried to keep near the living movement of the sources rather than merely reproducing their surface. If the work errs, I accept the burden.

No private system is being smuggled into these materials. No false reconciliation has been imposed upon traditions that genuinely differ.

The work nonetheless stands against modern fragmentation and reduction. It seeks to restore a wider field of intelligibility in which neglected dimensions of life may again be considered.

That restoration depends on the reader as well. Literal understanding is not enough. Subtle matters ask for an inward faculty able to receive implication and relation.

The book likewise depends on a larger community of help. Preservation, translation, editing, design, production, and support all contributed to its existence.

Gratitude therefore belongs to the structure of the work, not merely to the sentiment of its author.

If the reader leaves these pages able to perceive deeper relation, then the work will have fulfilled the logic of its own making.
