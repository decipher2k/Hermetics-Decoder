Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Form-in-Service
Visible shaping is justified when it remains answerable to the depth it carries.

2. Humility Before Fragments
Interpretation becomes trustworthy only when it remembers the incompleteness of its sources.

3. Shared Custody
Preservation passes through communities, institutions, craftsmen, and readers.

4. Widened Reason
The work does not reject reason; it resists any version of reason narrowed into reduction.

5. Symbolic Readiness
The reader must learn to meet texts as layered carriers of meaning, not as flat containers of information.

6. Gratitude as Recognition
Thankfulness here is not decorative feeling but acknowledgment of the real conditions of transmission.

Relation to the whole work
The final Preface cycle stabilizes the book's operating ethic: worthy form, bounded interpretation, collective stewardship, and symbolic participation. These principles govern every later chapter.
