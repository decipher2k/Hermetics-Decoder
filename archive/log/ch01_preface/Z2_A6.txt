Reformulation

This book presents itself not as possession but as stewardship. The hidden philosophies of antiquity have too often been transmitted in forms that fail to honor their depth. Here the attempt has been to prepare a more fitting vessel, one in which form serves meaning and beauty strengthens seriousness.

The finished manuscript belongs to years of writing, yet those years were only the outward phase of a longer service. Research, collection, comparison, and arrangement had to take place before the work could become visible. Footnotes were removed to preserve continuity, references were internalized, and aids to further study were kept in the bibliography and index.

I claim no exemption from error and no solitary originality. Ancient materials survive only in part, and responsible interpretation must remain aware of that limit. Some statements may challenge familiar expectation, but they have not been adopted casually. I have tried to remain faithful to the animating meaning of the sources without forcing them into lifeless literalism. Where the work falls short, I accept responsibility.

I have not made the material serve a private school of thought. Nor have I erased tensions among traditions for the sake of superficial agreement.

The work stands against materialist closure because it takes seriously dimensions of life that modern habits often exclude from consideration. Its purpose is not to deny reason, but to widen the field within which reason may operate.

To read such material well is a kind of apprenticeship. Language can suggest these realities, but not exhaust them. The reader must learn to recognize depth by more than statement alone.

This book also bears the trace of many helpers. Libraries, translators, editors, designers, printers, and subscribers each served the act of transmission.

Without their labor the work would have remained incomplete; without their trust it might never have reached public form.

I therefore record my gratitude not as courtesy, but as acknowledgment of fact.

If the reader leaves these pages with a broadened capacity to perceive hidden relation, the labor spent on them will have answered its purpose.
