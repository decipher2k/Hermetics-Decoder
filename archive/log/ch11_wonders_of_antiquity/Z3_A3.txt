Operative qualities

Reformulation

The chapter's three major domains now read as one continuum. The lamp is transmission in miniature. The oracle is transmission in living voice. The wonders are transmission in civilizational mass. The same law scales upward: hidden order secures a support adequate to its duration and range.

This scaling is important because it unifies the chapter's seemingly mixed materials. A prepared wick and a planetary monument belong to the same family of acts. Both stabilize relation between fleeting human life and a more enduring order.

Time is therefore central. The lamps challenge the consumption of time. Oracles interrupt ordinary succession with decisive utterance. Monuments stretch significance across generations. Hall's wonder-world is a world trying to give permanence to what would otherwise vanish.

The chapter can thus be read as a meditation on temporal rescue by form.
