Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Preserved Flame
Light can be prepared to survive burial, darkness, and the erosion of ordinary time.

2. Mediated Voice
Sacred utterance descends through arranged environments, purified bodies, and resonant instruments.

3. Monumental Memory
Architecture and sculpture store doctrine in durable visible form.

4. Initiated Craft
True makers are custodians whose labor joins technical exactness to symbolic intelligence.

5. Remnant Wonder
Marvel appears when function survives more fully than explanation.

6. Material Custodianship
Matter serves as archive, vessel, and threshold for hidden wisdom.

Relation to the whole work
This first cycle makes the chapter a major statement of the book's recurring claim that wisdom can outlive its original teachers by being sealed into objects, rites, and built forms that remain partially legible across centuries.
