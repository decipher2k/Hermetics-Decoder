Operative qualities
Preservative intelligence, embodied mystery, sacred technique, and durable transmission.

Convergence check
The chapter is converging toward a stable reading in which wonder is the surviving effect of forms engineered to preserve light, voice, and doctrine across time.

Reformulation

At this stage the chapter stabilizes around one claim: antiquity knew how to build preservative forms. Lamps preserved flame, oracles preserved inspired utterance, and monuments preserved symbolic order. The marvelous quality of these things arises because the original science of their construction has only partly survived.

The chapter is therefore not a miscellaneous anthology of marvels. It is a meditation on how hidden knowledge remains active in material supports long after explanatory traditions fragment.
