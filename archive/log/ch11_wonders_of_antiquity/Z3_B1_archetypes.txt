Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Archival Matter
Objects and structures can preserve living relations rather than inert information alone.

2. Transformative Remnant
The surviving sacred form still acts upon consciousness and posture.

3. Scaled Transmission
One law of preservation appears as lamp, oracle, and monument across increasing magnitudes.

4. Priestly-Artisan Custody
Hidden wisdom endures through the joint labor of interpreters, ritualists, and makers.

5. Nodal Geography
Sacred force is distributed through selected places that anchor intensified relations.

6. Re-enchanted Understanding
True interpretation restores the purpose of marvel without collapsing it into superstition or reduction.

Relation to the whole work
This final cycle makes the chapter a crucial bridge between earlier sacred cosmology and later technical-symbolic chapters by showing that wisdom survives historically through material archives, charged places, and collaborative custodianship.
