Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Consecrated Interface
Prepared matter becomes a threshold where hidden force can persist or enter manifestation.

2. Ceremonial Engineering
Ritual settings are designed systems rather than accidental scenes of inspiration.

3. Guarded Passage
Access to revelation occurs through controlled transitions, ordeals, and altered states.

4. Mnemonic Environment
Sacred knowledge survives in repeatable acts, charged places, and durable arrangements.

5. Planetary Inscription
Monumental works localize cosmic principles within geography and architecture.

6. Remnant Reconstruction
Later minds must infer principles from surviving effects.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle deepens the book's larger argument by showing that esoteric knowledge is often transmitted through consecrated environments and threshold mechanisms, not through doctrine abstracted from place, rite, and form.
