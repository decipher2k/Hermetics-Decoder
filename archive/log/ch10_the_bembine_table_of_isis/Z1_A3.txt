Z1_A3

Extraction

Central statements
- Ritual precision is treated as metaphysically necessary.
- Symbol, gesture, costume, and sequence are operative links in a chain of invocation.

Operative qualities
Precision, invocation, ceremonial exactitude, analogical contact, and transformative participation.

Reformulation

The chapter's theurgic doctrine makes ritual precision metaphysically necessary. A missing feature breaks the chain. Garments, insignia, bodily preparation, symbolic arrangement, sacrifice, chant, and image all matter because they create analogical contact between human action and divine pattern.

This gives ceremonial form an ontological seriousness. Rite is not theatrical display; it is structured participation in a higher order.
