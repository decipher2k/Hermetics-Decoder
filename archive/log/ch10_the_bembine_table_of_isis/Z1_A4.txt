Z1_A4

Extraction

Central statements
- The Table is organized by triads, panels, and emanative ordering.
- Unity diffuses into multiplicity without losing coherence.

Operative qualities
Emanation, triadic order, distributed unity, and structured descent.

Reformulation

The chapter repeatedly reads the Table through triads and zones. Threefoldness radiates outward: higher source, mediating orders, lower worlds; concealed crown, visible septenaries, distributed powers; center and surrounding hierarchies.

The point is not numerological ornament alone. The Table demonstrates how unity diffuses into multiplicity while retaining intelligible structure.
