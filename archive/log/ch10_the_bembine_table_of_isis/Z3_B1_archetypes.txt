1. Visible Concentration
A complex object can concentrate an entire science of order.

2. Restored Use
Symbols are understood rightly when returned to their ritual and doctrinal field.

3. Ceremonial Syntax
Exact correspondence is the grammar through which higher alignment is sought.

4. Centered Radiation
Multiplicity remains coherent when it is read as emanating from hidden source.

5. Scalable Analogy
One design can govern cosmos, body, altar, rite, and initiation across levels.

6. Initiatory Activation
Depth appears only when the object is entered as system rather than catalogued as surface.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle fixes the Tablet as one of the clearest demonstrations in the book that symbolic form can preserve doctrine, ritual method, hierarchical cosmology, and initiatory anthropology simultaneously. It is a master-example of concentrated esoteric literacy.
