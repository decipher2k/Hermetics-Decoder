Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Compressed Totality
A single object can condense an entire theological and cosmological system.

2. Operative Symbolism
Symbols act when correctly arranged, interpreted, and enacted.

3. Emanative Structure
Unity unfolds by ordered diffusion into triads, panels, and hierarchies.

4. Central Transmission
The hidden source becomes communicable through a mediating center.

5. Analogical Polyvalence
One structure can map cosmos, body, rite, initiation, and alchemy at once.

6. Initiatory Reading
Depth appears only when surface description yields to disciplined symbolic literacy.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle makes the Tablet one of the book's sharpest demonstrations that hidden wisdom can be stored in complex symbolic arrangements whose full force depends on ritual, interpretation, and analogical thought acting together.
