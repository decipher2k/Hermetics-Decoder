Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Preserved Operativity
Form can retain function after ceremonial life has faded.

2. Analogical Alignment
Ritual efficacy depends on exact correspondence across levels.

3. Counted Emanation
Number structures the descent and redistribution of power.

4. Centered Diffusion
Hidden plenitude unfolds through a mediating center into visible multiplicity.

5. Lawful Transposition
One design can map multiple scales without incoherence.

6. Structural Activation
Symbols become alive when read as an interrelated system rather than as isolated figures.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle sharpens the Tablet into a master-example of how the book's symbolic method works: visible complexity becomes intelligible when read as preserved operativity, analogical structure, and emanative order rather than as decorative excess.
