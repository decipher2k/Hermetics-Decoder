Central statements
- Polyvalent reading culminates as analogical scalability.
- The same form governs world, man, altar, rite, and inner transformation.

Operative qualities
Analogical scalability, multi-level coherence, rite-body-world continuity, and symbolic transfer.

Reformulation

The many readings of the Table culminate as analogical scalability. One form governs world, body, altar, rite, generation, and inner transformation because all are treated as scales of one coherent order. The Tablet is therefore not ambiguous so much as fertile within strict limits.
