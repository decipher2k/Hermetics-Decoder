Extraction

Central statements
- The central enthroned figure functions as transmitting universal principle.
- The whole arrangement flows outward from a hidden but fecund center.

Operative qualities
Central transmission, fecund source, all-containing mediation, and ordered unfolding.

Reformulation

The enthroned central figure is the chapter's decisive symbolic center. She functions as transmitting universal principle: the all-containing mediator through whom the hidden source unfolds itself into differentiated worlds, powers, and processes.

Everything else on the Table becomes intelligible in relation to this center. The object is organized as a radiating order rather than a scattered collage.
