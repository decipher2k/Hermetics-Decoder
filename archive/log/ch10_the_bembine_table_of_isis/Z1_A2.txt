Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter opposes descriptive antiquarianism with operative interpretation.
- The Tablet matters because it was used, not merely because it existed.

Operative qualities
Operative use, anti-antiquarian reading, symbolic efficacy, and awakened function.

Reformulation

The chapter repeatedly opposes surface description with operative interpretation. Measurements, materials, ownership history, and reproduction matter only secondarily. The deeper issue is what the Tablet was for.

The answer given is demanding: it was a ritual and doctrinal instrument, a visual synthesis of sacred correspondences, and a device through which priests aligned themselves with higher powers by exact ceremonial intelligence.
