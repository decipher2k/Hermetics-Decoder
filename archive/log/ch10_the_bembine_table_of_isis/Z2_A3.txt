Z2_A3

Extraction

Central statements
- Triadic and septenary arrangements clarify emanation and distribution.
- Number here structures intelligible descent.

Operative qualities
Numerical structure, emanation, ordered descent, and distributed hierarchy.

Reformulation

The chapter's repeated triads and septenaries now read as instruments of intelligible descent. Number is not ornamental. It structures the diffusion of source into worlds, powers, regions, and functions. Multiplicity is therefore counted because it is ordered.
