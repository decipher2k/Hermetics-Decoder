Extraction

Central statements
- Visible and invisible, spirit and form, are treated as layered rather than separate realities.
- Form exists within spirit, not outside it.
- The body is the outer house of a deeper and more extensive life.

Operative qualities
Containment, layered being, invisible causation, embodied residence, and interior precedence.

Reformulation

The chapter's metaphysic stabilizes around inclusion. Spirit is not a small occupant hidden inside a material shell. Spirit first circumscribes and includes form. The visible is therefore contained within the invisible, not the reverse.

This reversal matters for the whole symbolic system. The body is a house, robe, or vehicle of a deeper life, but that deeper life is more extensive than the form it animates. The universe itself stands in the same relation to the all-pervading spirit.

Thus bodily symbolism is not a materialist reduction of theology. It is the recognition that form manifests and localizes powers whose true scope exceeds the form.

The chapter therefore trains the reader to see embodiment as inclusion of deeper life, not imprisonment alone.
