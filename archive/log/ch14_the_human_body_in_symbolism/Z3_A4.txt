Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter treats human beings as hosts of many life-waves and orders.
- The body is a composite environment, not a simple unit.
- Embodiment is ecological as well as symbolic.

Operative qualities
Composite hosting, layered life, internal ecology, and plural embodiment.

Reformulation

Another mature line in the chapter is its treatment of the body as a composite environment. Mineral, plant, animal, human, and unseen life-orders are all said to unfold through or within the human constitution. The body is not a simple unit but a host-world.

This expands bodily symbolism into ecology. The microcosm contains many waves of becoming, just as the universe contains many orders of life.

The human being is therefore both individual and habitat, a nexus where diverse processes are gathered and advanced.

The chapter thereby deepens its account of man as universe in miniature.
