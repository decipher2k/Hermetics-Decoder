Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Inward Measure
The body provides a proportional standard by which larger realities are read.

2. Corrected Polarity
Symbolic oppositions require subtle reading rather than moral simplification.

3. Scalable Organism
Individual, communal, and cosmic bodies follow analogous formal laws.

4. Vestured Being
Form and adornment express degrees of manifestation and inward quality.

5. Partial Descent
Embodiment involves a limited entry of higher life into material dream.

6. Living Reversal
Initiation restores conscious relation to source without waiting for bodily death.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle deepens the book's central anthropological method by showing that the body is not only a symbolic index of the cosmos but also the operative site where descent, interpretation, and conscious return take place.
