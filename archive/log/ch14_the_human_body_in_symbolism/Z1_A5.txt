Extraction

Central statements
- Ritual architecture and bodily symbolism are directly related.
- Temples and initiatory chambers replicate the body's inner order.
- Initiation is a guided passage through embodied cosmology.

Operative qualities
Ritual embodiment, spatial analogy, guided ascent, chambered knowledge, and enacted symbolism.

Reformulation

The chapter also reveals why bodily symbolism matters institutionally. Temples were constructed as analogues of the human form, and the degrees of initiation were conferred in chambers corresponding to the body's centers. Spatial movement recapitulated inner development.

This means that initiation was not arbitrary ceremony. It was embodied cosmology enacted. The candidate entered a symbolic body and passed through its powers in ordered sequence.

The movement from lower chamber to higher chamber mirrored ascent through levels of cognition and finally to the heart-mystery concealed behind outward arrangements.

The chapter therefore shows how the human body supplied the blueprint for sacred architecture, ritual progression, and pedagogical design.
