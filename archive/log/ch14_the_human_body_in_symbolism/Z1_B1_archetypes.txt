Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Embodied Cosmos
The human constitution condenses universal law into readable form.

2. Symbol Versus Idolatry
Living emblems die when sign is mistaken for source.

3. Inclusive Spirit
Form exists within deeper life and not apart from it.

4. Triadic Centering
Heart, mind, and generative force stage a graded anthropology.

5. Ritual Blueprint
Body structure becomes the template for temple and initiation.

6. Regenerative Anatomy
The human form preserves pathways of awakening and reunion.

Relation to the whole work
This first cycle makes the chapter one of the book's central bridges between cosmology and practice by showing that the body is both symbolic archive and operative site of initiation.
