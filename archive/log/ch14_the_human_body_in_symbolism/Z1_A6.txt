Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter links regeneration to awakening hidden centers and meanings within the body.
- Bodily symbolism serves a practical soteriological aim.
- The human form is the site of liberation, not merely a subject of study.

Operative qualities
Regeneration, awakening, concealed capacity, embodied salvation, and transformative knowledge.

Reformulation

The body in this chapter is not only a symbol to be interpreted. It is the field in which liberation occurs. Ancient doctrine concealed practical knowledge about awakening, purifying, and reuniting the human being with its deeper source.

This gives the entire discussion a regenerative purpose. To understand the body rightly is to discover how man may pass from ignorance, bondage, and symbolic confusion toward illumination.

Garments, colors, organs, hands, limbs, sides, and inner diagrams all matter because they preserve techniques and maps of transformation.

The chapter therefore turns anatomy into operative theology: the body is where redemption becomes possible.
