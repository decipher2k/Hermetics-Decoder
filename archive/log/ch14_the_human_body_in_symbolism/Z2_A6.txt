Extraction

Central statements
- The body is the site where the divine part descends, dreams, and may awaken.
- Incarnation is partial and reversible.
- Initiation anticipates what death otherwise accomplishes.

Operative qualities
Descent, partial embodiment, awakening, reunion, and conscious reversal.

Reformulation

The chapter's regenerative teaching becomes more precise here. Only part of the higher nature descends into material dream. Birth is a temporary dissociation, death a rejoining, and initiation a conscious anticipation of that reunion without physical dissolution.

This gives bodily symbolism a dramatic role in the economy of salvation. The body is where the descending spark sleeps and where it may also awaken.

The Mysteries therefore become techniques for reversing forgetfulness while still alive.

The chapter culminates in this practical promise: embodiment need not end in imprisonment if its hidden relation to source is consciously restored.
