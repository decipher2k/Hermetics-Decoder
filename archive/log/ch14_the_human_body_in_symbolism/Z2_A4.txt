Extraction

Central statements
- Individual, collective, and cosmic bodies are structurally analogous.
- Communities, religions, races, and worlds are treated as composite organisms.
- The microcosmic logic scales upward into social and civilizational life.

Operative qualities
Scalability, composite being, social organism, nested individuality, and collective embodiment.

Reformulation

Another strong line of development is the scaling of body-logic beyond the individual. Religions, races, communities, and nations are each described as composite organisms made from many living units. The same formal logic that explains the human body is extended to collective bodies.

This expansion matters because it prevents microcosm from remaining merely personal. The chapter imagines reality as layered embodiment at many scales.

Each collective entity has a kind of body, identity, and developmental rhythm. The organismic model becomes universal.

The chapter therefore links personal anthropology to historical and communal interpretation.
