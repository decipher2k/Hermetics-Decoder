Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter's body symbolism trains a transferable mode of reading.
- Parts are read by function, proportion, relation, and degree.
- The body becomes a hermeneutic model for the whole book.

Operative qualities
Transferable reading, functional symbolism, proportional interpretation, and embodied hermeneutics.

Reformulation

By this point the chapter is teaching not only doctrine but method. The reader learns to interpret parts through their relations, degrees, functions, and positions. Limb, organ, center, side, and garment become readable by structural role rather than isolated appearance.

This mode of reading is transferable far beyond anatomy. It prepares the reader for symbols throughout the book by establishing the body as a hermeneutic model.

The chapter therefore acts as a training ground in correspondential interpretation.

Embodied symbolism becomes an education in how to read wholes through parts.
