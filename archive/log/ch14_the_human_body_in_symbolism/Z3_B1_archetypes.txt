Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Human Threshold
The body is the nearest accessible revelation of larger metaphysical order.

2. Universal Veil
Embodiment hides practical doctrine in forms so common they pass unnoticed.

3. Secret Heart-Center
Interior dignity corrects outward hierarchy and guides true ascent.

4. Composite Habitat
The human constitution hosts many layered orders of life and becoming.

5. Embodied Hermeneutics
The body's relations teach how symbols throughout the work should be read.

6. Living Sanctuary
Initiation turns the body from prison into site of conscious reunion with source.

Relation to the whole work
This final cycle makes the chapter one of the book's central interpretive keys: it explains why the human being can serve as microcosm, temple, symbol-system, and regenerative field for nearly every doctrine that follows.
