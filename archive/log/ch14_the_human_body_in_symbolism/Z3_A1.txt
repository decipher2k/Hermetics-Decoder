Central statements
- The chapter resolves into the body as the most accessible revelation of hidden order.
- Embodiment makes metaphysics experiential and teachable.
- Man is the practical threshold of theology.

Operative qualities
Accessible revelation, experiential metaphysics, pedagogical embodiment, and human threshold.

Reformulation

On the third pass, the chapter's deepest function appears in its practicality. The body is the nearest available revelation of hidden order. What theology, cosmology, and initiation seek to teach becomes experientially approachable because man carries their pattern within himself.

This makes anthropology the threshold of metaphysics. To study the body rightly is not to turn away from the divine, but to approach the divine through its most intimate condensation.

The chapter's insistence on man as textbook, measure, and temple all point toward this same pedagogical generosity.

The human form becomes the teachable edge of the infinite.
