Z3_A3

Extraction

Central statements
- The heart is increasingly revealed as the hidden center behind visible hierarchies.
- Exterior ranking is repeatedly corrected by interior dignity.
- True ascent moves toward inward center rather than upward display.

Operative qualities
Hidden center, interior dignity, corrected ascent, and concealed sovereignty.

Reformulation

The heart emerges with greater force here as the chapter's secret sovereign. Even when the brain occupies outward height, the heart retains inward priority. Visible hierarchies are therefore constantly being corrected by concealed centrality.

This is one of the chapter's most important spiritual instructions. Ascent is not simply movement toward a higher visible place; it is return toward inner source.

Such correction also explains why ancient teachings used blinds. Outward arrangements both guide and conceal the true center.

The chapter thus makes inward sovereignty the key to embodied symbolism.
