First reduction yields six governing operations.

1. Healing originally belonged to the temple and the initiatory sciences.
2. Disease begins in invisible disorder before it condenses physically.
3. Archæus and mumia mediate between life force and bodily form.
4. Therapeutics works by reharmonizing vehicles and redirecting vital currents.
5. Herbs, talismans, sound, prayer, regimen, and practical intervention are all legitimate when rightly integrated.
6. Medicine becomes harmful when it severs effect from cause and force from wisdom.

The chapter is therefore a philosophy of healing as restoration of harmony.
