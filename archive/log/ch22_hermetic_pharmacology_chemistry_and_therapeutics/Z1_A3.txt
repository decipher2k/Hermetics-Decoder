Primary symbolic clusters in pass one:

- Temple medicine and priestcraft as custodians of integrated knowledge.
- Paracelsus as restorer of Hermetic medicine.
- Archæus, astral light, etheric double, and mumia as mediating vehicles.
- Transference and substitution: diseased mumia moved into plant, animal, talisman, or medium.
- Seven causes and seven cures of disease.
- Herbalism, astrology, vibration, prayer, clairvoyance-inducing substances, and sacred waters.

These clusters unify around one thesis: healing requires access to subtle causation, not merely visible symptom-management.
