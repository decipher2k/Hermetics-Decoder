Third pass isolates the chapter's final center: healing is the restoration of relation across levels of being. The body is not a sealed machine but the last visible register of larger currents. When those currents are distorted, symptoms appear; when they are reordered, health returns.

This makes chapter 22 one of the book's most practical expressions of the microcosm doctrine. Medicine becomes a privileged field where cosmology, ethics, ritual, and embodiment visibly meet.
