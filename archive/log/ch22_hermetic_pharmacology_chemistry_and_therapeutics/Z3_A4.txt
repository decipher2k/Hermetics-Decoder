Final transmutation:

Hall's Hermetic therapeutics is not a collection of curious remedies but a coherent medical metaphysics. It assumes that life descends through subtle vehicles, that illness begins in distorted relation, and that cure requires the right medium to intercept, redirect, balance, purge, or strengthen the disturbed current. Herbs, charms, sounds, waters, diets, and prayers are all tools only because the human organism is layered and responsive at more levels than flesh alone.

The chapter therefore turns medicine into an art of correspondential restoration. The true physician reads disorder where it begins and works with Nature's own descending and ascending powers.
