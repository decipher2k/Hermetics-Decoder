What must be avoided:

- Treating the chapter as premodern quackery or mere occult curiosity.
- Missing its critique of fragmented knowledge.
- Flattening archæus and mumia into vague metaphors without operative force.
- Separating Hermetic pharmacology from the larger doctrine of harmony and correspondence.

What must be preserved:

- Integrated sacred science of healing.
- Invisible causation preceding bodily disease.
- Therapeutics as reharmonization.
- Multiple healing media united by correspondence.
