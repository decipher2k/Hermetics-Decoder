Final clarification of major symbols:

- Temple medicine: healing inside a unified wisdom tradition.
- Paracelsus: restorer of causal and symbolic breadth.
- Archæus and mumia: vital current and its transport-body.
- Seven causes and seven cures: patterned plurality of illness and remedy.
- Herbal and talismanic correspondences: material and astral sympathy joined.
- Sound and prayer: vibratory and devotional reordering of life.

These symbols are strongest when read as channels of restoration.
