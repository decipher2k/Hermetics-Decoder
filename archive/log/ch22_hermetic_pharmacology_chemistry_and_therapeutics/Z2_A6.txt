Hall uses Hermetic medicine to argue that healing must address the layered channels through which life enters the body. Archæus, mumia, etheric form, astral condition, mental attitude, karmic burden, celestial influence, and bodily state all participate in the genesis of disease. For that reason no single therapeutic medium is sufficient in principle. Herbs, sound, prayer, talismans, diet, astrology, and practical intervention each work by mediating or restoring a different relation.

The chapter's deeper force lies in its refusal to separate medicine from cosmology, ethics, and sacred anthropology.
