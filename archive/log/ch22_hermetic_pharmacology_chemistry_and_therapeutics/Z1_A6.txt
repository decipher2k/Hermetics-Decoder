Recomposition sketch:

Hall presents Hermetic medicine as a sacred science originally held within the temple and grounded in the unity of philosophy, religion, and natural knowledge. Disease arises first in the invisible constitution of man, especially in the etheric, astral, mental, or karmic dimensions, and only later manifests physically. Paracelsus is celebrated for recovering this view and for teaching that archæus, the universal life force, works through vehicles called mumia, whose disturbances underlie many bodily disorders.

The chapter's therapeutic range is correspondingly broad: herbs, talismans, sound, prayer, diet, astrology, and practical medicine all have their place insofar as they restore balance in the vehicles of life. Healing here means the reestablishment of harmony between levels of being.
