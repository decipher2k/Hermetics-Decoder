Second-pass clarification of special forms:

- Archæus: universal life current.
- Mumia: vehicle by which force, disease, or remedy is transferred.
- Etheric double: vulnerable pattern-body preceding physical expression.
- Herbal signatures: shape and planetary affinity as clues to correspondence.
- Sacred drugs and waters: intensifiers of perception and ritual transition.
- Chants and mantras: vibratory reordering of body and consciousness.

These forms show that Hermetic medicine is a science of patterned transmission.
