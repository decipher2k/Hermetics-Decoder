Central statements
- The macrocosm-microcosm law is now fixed as the practical center of the chapter.
- Cosmic order is redistributed through the human being.

Operative qualities
Practical analogy, redistributed cosmos, embodied participation, and interior mapping.

Reformulation

The macrocosm-microcosm law becomes the chapter's practical center. Cosmic order is redistributed through the human being, who carries in miniature the same patterns that govern the heavens. The zodiac is therefore not distant astronomy only; it is interior mapping.
