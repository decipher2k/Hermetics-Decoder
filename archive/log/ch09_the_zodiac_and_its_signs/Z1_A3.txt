Extraction

Central statements
- Precession gives the zodiac historical depth.
- Religious ages are linked to the shifting equinoctial position of the sun.

Operative qualities
Historical cycling, age-formation, equinoctial shift, and collective transformation.

Reformulation

The doctrine of precession gives the chapter its historical depth. The sun slowly regresses through the signs over immense periods, and each age takes on the symbolic character of the sign through which the vernal crossing occurs. Bull ages, ram ages, fish ages, and later water-bearing ages are therefore not merely astronomical descriptions but world-historical patterns.

The chapter's larger claim is that collective religious imagination changes with cosmic rhythm.
