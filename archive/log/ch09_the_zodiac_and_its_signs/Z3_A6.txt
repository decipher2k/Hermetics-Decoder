Central statements
- Soul-descent through zodiacal gates gives the chapter an initiatory anthropology.
- Celestial order becomes the pathway of incarnation and return.

Operative qualities
Initiatory anthropology, gated descent, sequential embodiment, and cosmic passage.

Reformulation

The chapter's final anthropological depth appears in its doctrine of soul-descent. The spirit passes through zodiacal gates, assumes symbolic conditions, and enters material embodiment through ordered celestial mediation. The heavens are thus not only a map of events, but a pathway of incarnation and eventual return.
