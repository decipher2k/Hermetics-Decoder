Extraction

Central statements
- Macrocosm and microcosm are directly linked through the zodiac.
- The body becomes a field of celestial correspondence.

Operative qualities
Analogy, embodied cosmos, distributed governance, and living correspondence.

Reformulation

The zodiac becomes anthropological when the chapter turns to the macrocosm and microcosm. The great world is read as a body, and the little world of man as its reflection. Signs govern bodily regions, planets govern ages and faculties, and the whole cosmos becomes readable through correspondence.

This is one of the chapter's strongest contributions to the book's general method.
