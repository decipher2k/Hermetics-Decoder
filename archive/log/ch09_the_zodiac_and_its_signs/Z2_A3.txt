Central statements

Operative qualities
Long-duration cycling, sacred history, collective atmosphere, and epochal symbolism.

Reformulation

Precession gives the chapter a vast historical scale. Ages are not simply dates; they are collective atmospheres in which certain symbolic forms become especially apt. As the equinoctial relation shifts, religions change emphasis, imagery, and sacrificial language accordingly.
