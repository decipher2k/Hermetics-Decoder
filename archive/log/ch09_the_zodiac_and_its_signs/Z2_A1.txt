Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter is best read as a theory of patterned celestial mediation.
- The zodiac orders relation rather than merely locating objects.

Operative qualities
Patterned mediation, ordered relation, temporal architecture, and symbolic governance.

Reformulation

The chapter increasingly reads as a theory of patterned celestial mediation. The zodiac does not merely place stars in an array. It organizes relations among time, power, embodiment, worship, and destiny. Its houses are therefore less containers than governing intervals of intelligible order.
