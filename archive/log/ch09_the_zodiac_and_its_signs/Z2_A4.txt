Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter uses age-symbolism to explain religious succession without randomness.
- Bull, ram, fish, and water-bearing motifs are serial manifestations of one moving order.

Operative qualities
Serial manifestation, age-conditioned worship, patterned succession, and symbolic adaptation.

Reformulation

The chapter's treatment of religious succession becomes sharper when read as serial manifestation. Bull, ram, fish, and water-bearing forms are not unrelated cult objects. They are successive symbolic accommodations to one moving celestial order.
