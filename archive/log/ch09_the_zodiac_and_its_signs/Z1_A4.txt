Z1_A4

Extraction

Central statements
- Zodiacal ages explain recurring shifts in worship forms and sacred imagery.
- Religious symbols are time-conditioned expressions of cosmic position.

Operative qualities
Age-symbolism, ritual adaptation, temporal sacrality, and pattern-conditioned worship.

Reformulation

The chapter uses zodiacal ages to explain why different epochs privilege different sacred creatures, sacrificial forms, and divine titles. Bull worship, lamb symbolism, fish imagery, and water-bearing figures are interpreted as age-conditioned expressions of the sun's relation to the celestial houses.

Religious symbolism therefore changes historically without becoming random.
