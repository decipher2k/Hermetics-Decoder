Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Living Pattern
Celestial order is treated as dynamically influential rather than mechanically remote.

2. Qualitative Naming
Symbols name operative powers, not merely visible shapes.

3. Age Formation
World history unfolds through long cyclic shifts in celestial emphasis.

4. Embodied Analogy
The body reflects the same order distributed across the heavens.

5. Double Hermeneutic
The same sign may bear exoteric and esoteric meanings depending on whether reflection or source is being read.

6. Descent Through Order
Soul, culture, and ritual all move within patterned celestial mediation.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle establishes the zodiac as one of the book's most comprehensive symbolic engines. It joins cosmology, religion, medicine, anthropology, and initiation into a single analogical framework.
