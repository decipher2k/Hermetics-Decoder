Z1_A6

Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter distinguishes exoteric and esoteric celestial readings.
- Reflection and source are not interpreted in the same way.

Operative qualities
Double reading, exoteric order, esoteric depth, reflective symbolism, and concealed source.

Reformulation

The chapter's distinction between geocentric and heliocentric astrology clarifies that celestial symbolism operates on more than one level. One reading concerns material and outward life; the other concerns higher intellectual and spiritual faculties. The same heavens can therefore be read exoterically as reflected influence or esoterically as concealed source.

This dual reading is central to the chapter's initiatory significance.
