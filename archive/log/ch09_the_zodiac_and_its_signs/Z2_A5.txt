Central statements
- Macrocosm and microcosm become one readable structure through zodiacal distribution.
- The body is a local theater of cosmic order.

Operative qualities
Readable analogy, local cosmos, bodily governance, and distributed participation.

Reformulation

The body becomes a local theater of cosmic order. Zodiacal distribution across bodily members and planetary distribution across life stages show that the human being is a concentrated expression of wider celestial structure. The law of analogy becomes practical rather than merely speculative.
