Extraction

Central statements
- The zodiac is treated as symbolic convention grounded in operative quality.
- The signs name powers, intensities, and relations rather than visual likeness alone.

Operative qualities
Symbolic convention, qualitative reading, operative naming, and patterned differentiation.

Reformulation

The chapter repeatedly insists that the zodiacal creatures are conventional but not arbitrary. Their value lies not in visual resemblance to the stars but in their ability to name powers, tendencies, seasonal qualities, and modes of solar action.

This makes the zodiac a disciplined symbolic language. The signs are interpretive tools for reading patterned differentiation across time and life.
