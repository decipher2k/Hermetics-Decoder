Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Ordered Interval
Celestial houses structure relations in time rather than merely mark positions in space.

2. Qualitative Operator
Signs encode modes of force, transition, and emphasis.

3. Epochal Atmosphere
Historical ages crystallize around shifting equinoctial relations.

4. Embodied Distribution
The same order is redistributed through organs, faculties, and life stages.

5. Initiatory Double Reading
The apparent sign may veil a deeper source-sign for the wise.

6. Serial Religious Form
Cultic symbols evolve as recurring celestial emphases change.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle sharpens the zodiac chapter into one of the book's central engines of symbolic historicity: it shows how visible order, temporal succession, bodily correspondence, and hidden teaching can all belong to one analogical science.
