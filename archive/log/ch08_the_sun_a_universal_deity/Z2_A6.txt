Extraction

Central statements
- The doctrine of the three suns clarifies layered causality.
- The visible world is effect, the intellectual world mediation, the spiritual world cause.

Operative qualities
Layered causality, mediation, reflection, inner sun, and hierarchical manifestation.

Reformulation

The three-suns doctrine now sharpens the chapter decisively. Material illumination belongs to the world of effects, intellectual illumination mediates, and spiritual illumination causes. The visible sun is meaningful precisely because it reflects higher order rather than replacing it.

This makes the chapter as anthropological as it is cosmological.
