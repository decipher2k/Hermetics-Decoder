Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter links religious forms across civilizations through the solar principle.
- Solar symbolism becomes a comparative key rather than a local cult detail.

Operative qualities
Comparative unification, trans-cultural recurrence, symbolic kingship, and luminous centrality.

Reformulation

The chapter uses the solar principle as a comparative key. Egyptian, Persian, Hindu, Chaldean, Greek, Norse, Masonic, Christian, and alchemical materials are drawn together because all of them preserve some version of radiant centrality, life-giving authority, cyclic victory over darkness, and visible mediation of invisible power.

This gives the chapter unusual breadth. Solar language is not confined to one nation or myth. It becomes a cross-civilizational grammar of sovereignty, order, sacrifice, and renewal.
