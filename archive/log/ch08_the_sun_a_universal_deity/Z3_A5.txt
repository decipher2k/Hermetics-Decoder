Central statements
- Christianity is fully framed as one later expression of solarized redemption-pattern.
- The chapter emphasizes continuity of structure rather than identity of doctrine.

Operative qualities
Structural continuity, redemption-pattern, transformed inheritance, and symbolic persistence.

Reformulation

The Christian material is best read as a later expression of the solarized redemption-pattern. The chapter does not collapse doctrines into sameness; it emphasizes continuity of structure. Luminous birth, saving labor, descent into darkness, return, and sacred calendrical timing persist because the pattern remains potent.
