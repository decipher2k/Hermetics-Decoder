Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Radiant Legibility
Visible recurrence renders hidden law readable.

2. Dynamic Trinity
Unity unfolds through recurring phases without loss of identity.

3. Salvific Cycle
Gift, labor, decline, concealment, and return form one regenerative pattern.

4. Symbolic Inheritance
Religions preserve luminous structures even when vocabularies change.

5. Layered Illumination
Material brightness is the outer sign of higher intellectual and spiritual light.

6. Inward Solarity
Human regeneration is the awakening of a hidden radiant center.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle fixes the solar chapter as one of the book's primary keys to symbolic interpretation: cosmic patterns are not merely observed, but translated into theology, anthropology, ritual, and initiatory transformation.
