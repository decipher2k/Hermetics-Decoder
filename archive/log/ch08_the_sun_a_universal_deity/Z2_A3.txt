Extraction

Central statements
- The Trinity here is processual, not static.
- Temporal movement grounds theological differentiation.

Operative qualities
Processual trinity, temporal manifestation, differentiation within unity, and rhythmic revelation.

Reformulation

The triune doctrine in this chapter is processual. One power manifests in distinct temporal phases without ceasing to be one. Rising, fullness, and withdrawal become the experiential basis for differentiated unity.

This gives the chapter's theology a rhythmic rather than merely conceptual character.
