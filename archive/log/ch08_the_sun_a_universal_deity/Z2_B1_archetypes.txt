Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Radiant Mediation
Visible light carries hidden causality into manifest life.

2. Rhythmic Trinity
Unity differentiates through temporal phases of emergence, fullness, and return.

3. Narrative Cycle
Cosmic recurrence generates heroic and salvific story-forms.

4. Adaptive Inheritance
Later traditions preserve earlier luminous structures under new language.

5. Hierarchical Reflection
Material brilliance is the lower expression of higher intellectual and spiritual suns.

6. Inward Solarity
Regeneration unfolds when the hidden center in man begins to radiate.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle deepens the chapter's role as a bridge-text. It links the book's concern with visible symbols to its deeper insistence that form, when read rightly, mediates higher law rather than merely decorating it.
