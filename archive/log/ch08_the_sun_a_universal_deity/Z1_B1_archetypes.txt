Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Visible Causality
The sun is the manifest sign of a higher life-giving source.

2. Triadic Unfolding
One power differentiates into source, manifestation, and return.

3. Heroic Cycle
Regeneration proceeds through twelvefold labor, decline, and renewal.

4. Symbolic Kingship
Radiant centrality becomes the pattern for authority, priesthood, and order.

5. Hierarchical Light
Material illumination reflects higher intellectual and spiritual radiance.

6. Comparative Continuity
Many religious systems preserve one recurrent solar grammar under different names.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle fixes the sun as one of the book's most powerful comparative engines. It unifies cosmology, mythology, ritual, kingship, and anthropology by showing how visible recurrence can bear witness to invisible law.
