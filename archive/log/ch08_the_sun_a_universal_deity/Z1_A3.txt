Extraction

Central statements
- The trinitarian doctrine is grounded in the observable phases of the sun.
- Daily solar rhythm becomes theology.

Operative qualities
Triadic unfolding, temporal revelation, visible theology, and cyclic differentiation.

Reformulation

The chapter grounds triune theology in the daily manifestation of the sun. Rising, midday, and setting become not merely times of day but a visible disclosure of origination, full manifestation, and descent. The three phases of the solar life make unity-in-differentiation thinkable.

This is one of the chapter's strongest claims: theology grows from patterned observation of cosmic process.
