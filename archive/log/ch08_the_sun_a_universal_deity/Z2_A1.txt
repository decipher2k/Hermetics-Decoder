Z2_A1

Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter is best read as a theory of visible mediation.
- The sun is the outward intelligible carrier of invisible beneficence and order.

Operative qualities
Visible mediation, beneficence, intelligibility, and radiant order.

Reformulation

The chapter increasingly reads as a theory of visible mediation. The sun is not ultimate source, yet it is the most compelling outward carrier of source-like power. It makes invisible beneficence intelligible through regular gift, radiance, and rule.
