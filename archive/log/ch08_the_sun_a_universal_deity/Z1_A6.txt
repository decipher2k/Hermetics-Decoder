Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter distinguishes spiritual, soular, and material suns.
- Visible light is effect; invisible light is cause.

Operative qualities
Layered causality, mediation, inner illumination, and hierarchical light.

Reformulation

The doctrine of the three suns clarifies the chapter's metaphysical depth. The material sun governs bodily life, the soular sun mediates illumination and redemption, and the spiritual sun is the causal source of wisdom and being. Visible radiance is therefore an effect or reflection of higher light.

The chapter thus moves from cosmology into anthropology: what the sun is outwardly, spirit is inwardly.
