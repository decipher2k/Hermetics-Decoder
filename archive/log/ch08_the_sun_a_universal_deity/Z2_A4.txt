Extraction

Central statements
- The twelvefold solar path is a pattern-generator for sacred narrative.
- Heroic religion is seasonal intelligibility rendered dramatically.

Operative qualities
Pattern-generation, seasonal heroism, dramatic intelligibility, and cyclical labor.

Reformulation

The twelvefold path now appears as a generator of sacred narrative. The heroic figures associated with the sun are not arbitrary inventions. They dramatize seasonal intelligibility: labor, beneficence, conflict, decline, concealment, and return rendered in personified form.
