Extraction

Central statements
- Christianity is interpreted as inheriting and recasting solar symbolism.
- Christological imagery is read through celestial pattern.

Operative qualities
Inheritance, recasting, salvific radiance, cyclic birth, and symbolic continuity.

Reformulation

The chapter argues that Christianity inherits and recasts solar symbolism at a structural level. Birth in darkness, radiant youth, redemptive labor, death, return, feast days, virgin motherhood, and the victory of light over darkness are all placed in continuity with earlier celestial patterns.

This is not presented as mere borrowing, but as persistence of a deeper symbolic grammar.
