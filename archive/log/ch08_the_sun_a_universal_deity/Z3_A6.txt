Reformulation

The doctrine of the three suns fixes the inward application of the chapter. Outer solar symbolism culminates in the regeneration of the hidden center in man. Material, intellectual, and spiritual light are coordinated, and the human being becomes a miniature solar field capable of reflecting or radiating higher order.
