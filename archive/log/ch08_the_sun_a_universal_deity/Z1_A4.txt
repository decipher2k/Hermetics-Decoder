Extraction

Central statements
- Annual and zodiacal movement make the sun the archetype of heroic labor and regenerative passage.
- The twelvefold path organizes mythic figures and sacred narratives.

Operative qualities
Twelvefold passage, heroic labor, seasonal transformation, and cyclic endurance.

Reformulation

The chapter repeatedly interprets the sun's annual course as heroic labor. Passing through the twelve houses, waxing, governing seasons, weakening, descending, and returning, the solar power becomes the model for myths of struggle, beneficent works, sacrificial decline, and victorious reemergence.

The twelvefold path is therefore not astronomical detail alone; it is narrative structure.
