This chapter presents The Chemical Marriage as a carefully staged mystery drama. Hall reads the seven days as ordered thresholds through which the candidate passes from invitation and rescue to weighing, sacrifice, alchemical transformation, and final commission. Virgo Lucifera governs many of these transitions as the intelligence of initiation itself, while the tower scenes turn symbolic marriage into exact regenerative process. Bodies are dissolved, reduced, re-formed, nourished, and reensouled until the new king and queen emerge as figures of restored intelligence and love. The narrative's real subject is not literary fantasy but the ritual making of a fit vessel for divine union.
