Extraction

Central statements
- Sea-born teachers and migrating symbols are the chapter's mechanism of continuity.
- The lost source is remembered through emissaries and repeated forms.

Operative qualities
Emissaries, repeated forms, migratory symbols, and broken continuity.

Reformulation

The chapter's theory of continuity is emissarial. After the center falls, priests, sages, and symbols travel. The culture-bringer rising from the sea, the feathered serpent, the sacred cross, the serpent emblem, the pyramid, and the holy city are all treated as repeated forms carrying broken continuity from a forgotten source.

The chapter is therefore not interested in origin as static beginning. It is interested in how origins keep acting after disappearance.
