Central statements
- The chapter's rituals train readers to reinterpret destruction as transformation.

Operative qualities
Transformative destruction, ritual mourning, sacred knowledge, and regenerative reversal.

Reformulation

One of the chapter's strongest shifts is its revaluation of destruction. Submergence, death, mutilation, descent, and suspension are not final negations. Within sacred understanding they become transitional forms. Mourning is therefore not despair alone; it is part of sacred knowledge because it keeps relation to restoration alive.

This explains why the dying-god rituals are so central. They train participants to read loss transformatively.
