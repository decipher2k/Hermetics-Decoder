Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Lost Center
A primordial center of order can disappear physically while remaining active symbolically.

2. Moral Catastrophe
Civilizational destruction follows inward corruption before outward collapse.

3. Scattered Remnant
Wisdom survives ruin by dispersing into symbols, priesthoods, and migrating myths.

4. Ritualized Fall
The dying god compresses cosmic and human fall into ceremonial drama.

5. Regenerative Memory
Rites preserve the memory of restoration even when the original world is gone.

6. Multi-Scale Pattern
The same structure governs continent, civilization, soul, and initiate: order, fall, concealment, and return.
