Extraction

Central statements
- The fall of Atlantis and the fall of consciousness mirror one another.
- Civilizational catastrophe is interiorized in the dying-god rites.

Operative qualities
Mirrored fall, interiorization, civilizational allegory, and ritual interiority.

Reformulation

Atlantis and the dying god mirror one another. The submerged continent is the outward image; the slain, buried, stripped, or suspended god is the inward image. In both, a prior order is lost, concealed, or broken, yet not finally destroyed.

This gives the chapter its full comparative coherence. Civilizational catastrophe is interiorized in ritual. What happened to the world in myth happens to the soul in initiation.
