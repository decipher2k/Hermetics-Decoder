Central statements

Operative qualities
Preserved origin, symbolic migration, vanished source, and durable recurrence.

Reformulation

The chapter's final center is preserved origin through symbolic migration. Atlantis functions as the name for a vanished concentration of sacred order whose disappearance does not end its influence. Instead, it continues through repeated sacred forms dispersed across cultures.

The question is therefore not whether Atlantis simply existed, but how a lost origin remains effective after ruin.

The chapter answers: through symbols, rites, myths, architecture, and emissaries strong enough to preserve pattern after place is gone.
