Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter compares myths by function rather than by surface narrative.
- The stable function is regenerative passage.

Operative qualities
Functional comparison, regenerative passage, symbolic stripping, and recovered life.

Reformulation

The chapter is most intelligible when its myths are compared by function. Ishtar's stripping, Adonis's death, Atys's suffering, Cabiric murder and recovery, and Odin's suspension all stage regenerative passage. Something higher is obscured, bound, wounded, or sacrificed; through the rite it becomes recoverable.

The stable function across traditions is not identical doctrine but enacted regeneration.
