Central statements
- Atlantis becomes a doctrine of involution.
- The lost continent symbolizes descent from archetypal order into mutable existence.

Operative qualities
Involution, archetypal order, descent, and mutable existence.

Reformulation

Atlantis is increasingly interpretable as a doctrine of involution. The sinking continent signifies more than disaster: it marks the descent of ordered consciousness into the unstable medium of temporal existence. The sacred mountain, central sanctuary, and concentric zones express a centered order that later becomes immersed.

This same involutionary pattern is echoed in the myths of descent that follow. Civilizational loss and soul-descent mirror one another.
