Reformulation

The chapter's recurring symbols function as conservation devices. The cross, serpent, sacred mountain, holy city, sea-born teacher, world tree, and buried or resurrected god all preserve memory on two levels at once: historical remembrance of broken transmission and initiatory remembrance of descent and return.

This layered symbolic economy is how the chapter explains continuity across cultures without requiring literal uniformity.
