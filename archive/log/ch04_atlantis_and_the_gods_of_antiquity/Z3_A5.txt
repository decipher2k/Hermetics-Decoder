Reformulation

The chapter strongly implies that later esoteric traditions depend on fragments of an older grammar of fall and regeneration. These fragments survive more reliably as pattern than as complete explanation. Ritual sequence, emblem, sacred object, calendrical timing, and death-and-return structures outlast systematic doctrine.

This makes the chapter foundational for the later book, which repeatedly traces hidden continuities through exactly such fragments.
