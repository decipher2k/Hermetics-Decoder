Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Active Origin
A source can disappear materially while remaining active symbolically.

2. Catastrophic Redistribution
Ruin does not end transmission; it scatters it.

3. Regenerative Passage
Death, descent, and stripping become preconditions of restored life.

4. Ritual Memory
Ceremony remembers what history forgets.

5. Structural Inheritance
Later religions inherit pattern even where explanation changes.

6. Multi-Scale Involution
The same descent governs continent, culture, soul, and god-image.
