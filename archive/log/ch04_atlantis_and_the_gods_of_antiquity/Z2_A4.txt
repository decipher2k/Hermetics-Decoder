Central statements
- Dying-god myths are now clarified as ritual technologies of remembering restoration.

Operative qualities
Ritual remembering, sacred mourning, seasonal return, and disciplined regeneration.

Reformulation

The dying-god section now reads more clearly as a ritual technology of remembering restoration. Tammuz, Adonis, Atys, and the others are not only lamented; they are ceremonially lost so that rebirth can be made participatory. Mourning becomes disciplined memory of return.

The initiate does not merely hear that life overcomes death. He passes through the drama that makes this claim experientially real.
