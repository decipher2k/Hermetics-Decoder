Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Vanished Center
A sacred origin may be destroyed as place while continuing as pattern.

2. Symbolic Diffusion
Catastrophe redistributes wisdom across cultures rather than annihilating it.

3. Ritual Interiorization
The dying god turns cosmic loss into initiatory experience.

4. Regenerative Mourning
Lamentation becomes disciplined relation to restoration.

5. Pattern Over Doctrine
What persists most durably is formal sequence rather than complete explanation.

6. Memory-Bearing Form
Myth survives because it stores processes that can be re-entered.
