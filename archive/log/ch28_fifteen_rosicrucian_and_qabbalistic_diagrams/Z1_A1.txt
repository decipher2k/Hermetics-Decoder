This chapter treats the fifteen diagrams as compressed doctrinal machines rather than illustrations. Hall reads them as Rosicrucian and Qabbalistic schemata of emanation, element, fall, restoration, sacred number, and cosmic structure.

Its main movement runs from primordial fiery water through elemental differentiation, Luciferian inversion, cosmic layering, and the symbolic restoration of order through diagrams of creation, judgment, regeneration, and divine correspondence.
