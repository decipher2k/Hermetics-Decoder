This chapter uses the fifteen diagrams as condensed vehicles of Rosicrucian and Qabbalistic science. Hall reads them as maps of stratified reality in which divine outflow, elemental differentiation, Luciferian inversion, sacred number, and human regeneration are coordinated through geometry. Their significance lies in their capacity to hold many relations at once: cosmological, scriptural, alchemical, angelic, and anthropological. The diagrams teach that symbolic truth often survives most powerfully in architectonic form, where hierarchy and correspondence can be grasped simultaneously instead of sequentially.
