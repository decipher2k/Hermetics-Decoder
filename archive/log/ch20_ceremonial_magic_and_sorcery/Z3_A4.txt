Final transmutation:

Hall's treatment of ceremonial magic is a study in how sacred technology fails when detached from inward transformation. The operator seeks to use names, forms, timings, and symbols as levers on the invisible, but if desire remains unpurified the whole procedure bends downward. The rite still works in a sense, yet it works against the soul that performs it. What was meant to order becomes a machinery of compulsion, fear, and dependence.

Thus the chapter turns demonology into anthropology. The real danger is not merely what answers the invocation, but the condition in the invocant that makes such an answer attractive.
