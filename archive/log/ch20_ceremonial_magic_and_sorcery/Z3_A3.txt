Final clarification of major symbols:

- Ceremonial apparatus: formal mediation requiring consecrated use.
- Atlantean-Egyptian corruption: sacred knowledge centralized into domination.
- Spirit pact: exchange that conceals irreversible asymmetry.
- Circle and sword: protected threshold before unstable forces.
- Inverted pentagram: human form reversed into lower governance.
- Vampire and werewolf motifs: appetite consuming others to sustain a doomed self.

These symbols are strongest when read as moral dynamics rather than occult curiosities.
