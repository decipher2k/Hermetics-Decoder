Primary symbolic clusters in pass one:

- Ceremonial apparatus: robes, wand, seals, circles, pentacles, swords, planetary timings.
- Corrupt priestcraft: Atlantis, Egypt, mutilated Mysteries, idolatry, domination through false interpretation.
- Pact logic: bargain, delay of death, inversion of mastery, soul pledged for force.
- Elementals and astral beings: morally variable intelligences conditioned by the operator.
- Black magic as selfishness: occult power reduced to appetite, domination, prolongation, acquisition.
- Inverted pentagram: power reversed from ascent to descent.

These clusters unify around one thesis: symbolism without virtue becomes predation.
