First reduction yields six governing operations.

1. Ritual form can mediate invisible power.
2. Without ethical mastery, mediation becomes corruption.
3. The desire to command spirits often masks ungoverned appetite.
4. Institutional religion can become organized sorcery when symbols are severed from wisdom.
5. Pacts with lower powers reverse mastery into servitude.
6. True completion requires inner transformation, not external coercion.

The chapter is therefore less a manual than a critique of premature transcendental ambition.
