Second-pass clarification of special forms:

- Magic circle: protected boundary, but also confession that the operator is unsafe before what he invokes.
- Sword and vestments: ritual concentration of authority and guarded intention.
- Planetary seals and pentacles: precise symbolic mediators vulnerable to misuse.
- Inverted pentagram: human ascent fallen back into animal or subhuman governance.
- Demon contract: externalized form of inward self-betrayal.
- Occult vampire and werewolf motifs: appetite unbound from rightful human form.

These symbols show that ceremonial magic is really a drama of boundaries and their breach.
