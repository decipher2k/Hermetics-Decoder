What must be avoided:

- Reading the chapter as practical instruction.
- Treating white and black magic as merely different spell systems.
- Missing the continuity between political tyranny, false theology, and occult corruption.
- Reducing the chapter to sensational demonology.

What must be preserved:

- The primacy of moral qualification.
- Ritual as a real but dangerous mediation of force.
- Selfishness as the root of black magic.
- Inversion as the key symbolic logic of corruption.
