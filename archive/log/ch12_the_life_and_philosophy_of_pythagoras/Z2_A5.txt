Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter treats immortality as ascent through purification and resemblance.
- Transmigration and spiritualization belong to one regenerative logic.
- Human destiny is measured by what one becomes like.

Operative qualities
Ascent, purification, resemblance, regeneration, and destiny through likeness.

Reformulation

The doctrines of transmigration, spiritualized body, and return to the immortals reveal a consistent law of resemblance. What the soul becomes like determines its state and its path. Bestial desire degrades; philosophical purification elevates.

Even where the chapter uses animal or reincarnational language, its deeper point is qualitative rather than sensational. One lives into the form of one's dominant affinities. Destiny is shaped by interior likeness.

This gives immortality moral and initiatory meaning. The ascent is not automatic. It requires purification of life, clarification of intellect, and restored participation in the Monad's order.

Pythagorean salvation, in this sense, is return through resemblance.
