Extraction

Central statements
- Number and geometry are treated as formative keys, not neutral abstractions.
- Mathematical order grounds ethics, cosmology, and education.
- To study number is to study relation itself.

Operative qualities
Proportion, intelligible form, exactness, formative pattern, and structural insight.

Reformulation

The chapter gives unusual authority to number and geometry because they disclose relation without distortion. Pythagoras requires mathematical competence before discipleship because mathematics teaches the soul to recognize proportion, order, and necessity.

This is why the triangular division of reality, the tetractys, the sacred solids, and the symbolic dignity of numbers matter so much. They are not curiosities added to philosophy. They are the very grammar by which the universe becomes thinkable.

Ethics also depends on this grammar. Excess is vice because it violates proportion. Disorder in the city, the household, the belly, and the soul all reflect loss of measure.

The chapter thus treats mathematics as spiritual discipline. To understand number rightly is to become aligned with the architecture of reality.
