Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. The Philosopher-Initiate
Wisdom is pursued through whole-life formation rather than detached speculation.

2. The Seeking Mind
True philosophy begins in disciplined love of wisdom, not premature possession.

3. The Monad and Its Orders
Unity distributes itself through living hierarchies without ceasing to be one.

4. Numerical Formation
Number and geometry teach the soul how relation and proportion govern reality.

5. Graded Transmission
Silence, secrecy, and degrees protect and shape genuine understanding.

6. Embodied Harmony
Ethics, diet, speech, healing, and friendship translate doctrine into lived consonance.

Relation to the whole work
This first cycle places Pythagoras at the book's hinge between archaic mystery transmission and explicit philosophical method, showing how hidden wisdom becomes educational form without losing its initiatory depth.
