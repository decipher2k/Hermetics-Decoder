Extraction

Central statements
- The chapter treats community itself as a vessel of wisdom.
- Doctrine survives persecution through bonds of recognition and fidelity.
- Fellowship becomes continuity under historical rupture.

Operative qualities
Fidelity, brotherhood, continuity, shared sign, and transmission through persons.

Reformulation

The Crotona school and the stories of surviving disciples show that Pythagorean philosophy is preserved as much by fellowship as by teaching-content. Signs on an inn door, common debts, reverence for the Master, and continued promulgation by his wife all reveal a community bound by inner recognition.

This matters because the chapter also narrates destruction. Buildings burn, persecution follows, manuscripts scatter, and the institution dissolves. Yet the bond persists.

Wisdom survives here through persons who carry signs, habits, memories, and obligations after visible structure is broken.

The chapter therefore reinforces one of the book's strongest historical laws: transmission often outlives institutions by moving into brotherhood and living fidelity.
