Extraction

Central statements
- Astronomy in the chapter is philosophical before it is physical.
- Heavenly bodies are focal points of intelligence and dignity.
- The cosmos is read as living order rather than inert mechanism.

Operative qualities
Living astronomy, celestial intelligence, symbolic cosmology, and ordered motion.

Reformulation

The astronomical material becomes clearer when read as philosophical astronomy. Pythagoras is not primarily rearranging celestial mechanics for curiosity's sake. He is presenting the cosmos as an ordered field of living intelligences centered on divine fire.

The ten spheres, the hidden counter-earth, the reverence for Venus, and the geometric imagination surrounding planetary form all point toward one claim: the heavens disclose intelligible structure because they are themselves expressions of intelligence.

Physical astronomy is secondary, the science of shadows. What matters most is that celestial arrangement reveals dignity, relation, and hierarchical order.

The chapter therefore turns the sky into a theological and initiatory diagram.
