Extraction

Central statements
- Healing, music, astronomy, and mathematics are coordinated as arts of restoring order.
- Knowledge is therapeutic because disharmony is a form of illness.
- The wise life heals by re-proportioning the soul and world.

Operative qualities
Therapeutic harmony, restoration, re-proportioning, integrative science, and ordered wellbeing.

Reformulation

The chapter reaches fuller coherence when its sciences are seen as therapeutic. Music heals disease, plants assist balance, diet preserves clarity, number corrects confusion, and astronomy relocates the soul within a larger order. Each field restores proportion where disorder had entered.

This makes Pythagorean knowledge medicinal in the broadest sense. Ignorance, luxury, sedition, discord, and excess are all illnesses of relation.

The philosopher becomes physician not because he abandons metaphysics, but because metaphysics teaches the pattern of health.

The chapter thereby unifies science, ethics, and spirituality under the sign of restored harmony.
