Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Translator of the Mysteries
Hidden tradition becomes explicit pedagogy without losing sacred depth.

2. Exemplary Mastery
Authority awakens disciplined seeking rather than passive dependence.

3. Numerical Consciousness
Reality is approached through structured relation, proportion, and triadic intelligibility.

4. Fraternal Continuity
Wisdom survives through fellowship, signs, and fidelity when institutions break.

5. Therapeutic Knowledge
Science, ethics, and spiritual practice heal by restoring harmony.

6. Initiatory Reason
The intellect is expanded by mystery and disciplined into symbolic understanding.

Relation to the whole work
This final cycle makes Pythagoras a decisive pivot in the book: the figure through whom ancient mysteries become educational method and the cosmos becomes readable through number, ethical discipline, and symbolic reason.
