Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Soul-Tuning
Education aims at resonance with truth rather than mere accumulation of concepts.

2. Symbolic Compression
Figures and aphorisms condense transformative instruction into memorable forms.

3. Ethical Cognition
Self-government is a prerequisite for seeing order clearly.

4. Microcosmic Correspondence
Human nature mirrors cosmic structure and becomes readable through it.

5. Destiny by Resemblance
The soul rises or falls according to the qualities with which it identifies.

6. Living Heaven
Astronomy discloses a cosmos of intelligences, dignities, and hierarchical motion.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle clarifies that Pythagoreanism supplies one of the book's most important methods: symbolic, ethical, and numerical education as a means of bringing human consciousness into accord with a living cosmos.
