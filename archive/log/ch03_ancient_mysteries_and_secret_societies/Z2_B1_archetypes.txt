Z2_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Threshold Management
Initiatory institutions exist to regulate passage from ordinary standing into stronger relation with truth.

2. Tested Readiness
Readiness is moral, intellectual, and affective; it must be proven, not presumed.

3. Embedded Transformation
Teachings endure by being built into sequence, symbol, role, space, and ordeal rather than left as abstract statements alone.

4. Hollowing Risk
The great danger of every sacred body is that form outlives force and ceremony outlives understanding.

5. Residual Architecture
Even after doctrinal thinning, ritual architectures can persist and continue transmitting fragments of older initiatory science.

6. Structural Inheritance
What later orders inherit most reliably is the pattern of transformation, not always the original explanation.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle sharpens the chapter's usefulness for the rest of the book. It explains how hidden wisdom can remain historically active even when its full original doctrine has been partially forgotten.
