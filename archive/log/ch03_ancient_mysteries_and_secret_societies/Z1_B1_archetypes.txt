Z1_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Graded Disclosure
Truth is distributed according to capacity, with visible narratives for the many and deeper interpretation for the prepared.

2. Transformative Ordeal
Knowledge becomes trustworthy only when joined to testing, fear, endurance, and moral proof.

3. Ritual Anthropology
Myths and ceremonies describe the condition of the soul: captivity, fragmentation, struggle, recollection, and release.

4. Institutional Fragility
No sacred body remains pure automatically; decline in character hollows out its forms.

5. Durable Residue
Even after corruption or suppression, symbols, grades, implements, and dramatic structures preserve fragments of older wisdom.

6. Adapted Continuity
Later fraternities may not inherit full doctrines, but they often inherit ritual architectures and anthropological assumptions from the ancient schools.

Relation to the whole work
This chapter converts the general claim of hidden wisdom into concrete institutional history. It gives the book its first full comparative model of how teachings are preserved, degraded, adapted, and partially transmitted through ritual forms.
