Z3_B1 Archetype Fork

1. Guarded Transformation
The core function of the Mysteries is not secrecy in itself but the protected transformation of the candidate.

2. Developmental Sequence
Truth is unfolded in stages because human receptivity is developmental.

3. Dramatized Doctrine
Myth and ceremony preserve teachings by making them experiential and repeatable.

4. Institutional Fragility
Every initiatory body risks becoming a shell if ritual survives without inward force.

5. Residual Transmission
Fragments of ancient wisdom persist in later ceremonial forms because architecture can endure beyond explanation.

6. Comparative Mechanism
The deepest continuity among ancient schools lies in shared ritual mechanism, not in uniform theology.

Relation to the whole work
This cycle fully integrates the chapter into the larger project of the book: hidden wisdom persists across history because it is encoded in symbols, institutions, and ritual designs that survive interruption better than explicit doctrine alone.
